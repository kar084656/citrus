Ingredients

  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 ⅓ cupsplain Greek yogurt
  • 2shallots
  • crushed red pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the naan.)
  2. Wash and dry the spinach (skip if it came pre-washed). Roughly chop and place in a medium bowl; set aside.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. Peel and mince garlic and shallots.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 shallots
  4. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. While the skillet heats up, drain and mince artichokes.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic, shallot, and salt; cook, stirring often, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Continue to cook, while stirring, until spinach is completely wilted, 1-2 minutes more. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  8. When the spinach is done, remove from heat. Stir in the artichokes and set aside to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  9. Meanwhile, place yogurt, salt, and crushed red pepper in the reserved bowl. Whisk until smooth.
    1 ⅓ cups plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  10. Add the cooled spinach-artichoke mixture to the bowl with the yogurt. Stir to combine the dip and set aside.
  11. Arrange naan on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.) Cut into wedges.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  12. To serve, divide toasted naan and dip between plates. Enjoy!
