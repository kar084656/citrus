Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F and coat a large baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. Meanwhile, preheat a small saucepan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add broth, tomato paste, and spices to the pan. Bring to a simmer, whisking frequently, and cook until slightly thickened, 3-4 minutes. Remove sauce from the heat and set aside.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    3 tbsp tomato paste
    ½ tsp cumin
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. Meanwhile, drain and rinse the beans in a colander.
    2 (15 oz) cans black beans
  5. Place beans and spices in a medium bowl. Crush with a fork or potato masher until almost smooth, with some texture remaining; set aside.
    1 tsp cumin
    1 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp onion powder
  6. Coarsely grate cheddar.
    ½ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  7. Place half the tortillas on the baking sheet and spread with bean filling; place remaining tortillas on top, pressing gently to adhere. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    8 small flour tortillas
  8. Spoon sauce over the tortillas and top with cheese. Place in the oven and bake until the cheese is melted and bubbly, 8-10 minutes.
  9. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 limes
    4 tomatoes
    1 small bunch cilantro
  10. Juice limes into the reserved bowl and add salt.
    1 tsp salt
  11. Small dice tomatoes. Peel and small dice onion; add both to the bowl with the lime.
    1 medium red onion
  12. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the pico de gallo.
  13. Remove pizzas from the oven and cut into quarters.
  14. Divide pizzas between plates; top with pico de gallo and yogurt. Enjoy!
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
