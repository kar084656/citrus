Ingredients

Steps

  1. Drain and rinse the beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  2. Wash the spinach (skip if it came pre-washed) and roughly chop.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. Crack the eggs into a medium bowl and season with salt and pepper; whisk together.
    6 eggs
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  4. Preheat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. Add spinach to the skillet and season with salt and pepper; cook, tossing frequently, until the spinach has wilted, 1-2 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Add beans to the skillet and stir to combine; add the egg mixture. Scramble the eggs using a combination of stirring and flipping, making sure to bring in all the mixture from the edges of the pan; cook until the eggs are no longer runny.
  7. Grate cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. To assemble the quesadillas, lay the tortillas on a flat surface and divide cheese and egg mixture between them, arranging it on one half of the tortilla; fold in half.
    4 large flour tortillas
  9. Wipe skillet clean and return to heat. Working in batches, drizzle the skillet with oil, and lay the assembled quesadillas in the pan. Cook until golden on both sides, 5-6 minutes, turning halfway through.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. To serve, cut quesadillas into wedges and divide between plates. Enjoy!
