Ingredients

  • 2 small bunchesasparagus
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ½ lblean ground beef
  • ½ lbwhite mushrooms
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • all-purpose flour
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • onion powder
  • salt
  • Worcestershire sauce

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb white mushrooms
    2 small bunches asparagus
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. Slice the mushrooms and transfer to a medium bowl.
  3. Peel and small dice the onion; add to the bowl of mushrooms.
    1 medium yellow onion
  4. Fill a medium saucepan (one that is big enough to fit the asparagus) about halfway with water; cover and bring to a boil.
  5. Peel and mince the garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. In a medium bowl, combine and mix together the minced garlic, ground beef, onion powder, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper. Shape the beef mixture into four oval patties.
    1 ½ lb lean ground beef
    1 tsp onion powder
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  8. Cut (or snap off) and discard the woody ends of the asparagus; leaves the spears on the cutting board.
  9. Add butter to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. Place the beef patties in the skillet and cook until they are browned and the meat is nearly cooked through, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  11. Once the water is boiling, add the asparagus. Cook until the asparagus is tender and bright green, 3 to 4 minutes. Drain in a colander and rinse under cold water to stop the cooking process; set aside to drain.
  12. Once the patties have been transferred, add more butter to the skillet.
    4 tbsp butter, unsalted
  13. Add the onion and mushrooms and season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, until the onions and mushrooms are softened and lightly browned, 3 to 5 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  14. Sprinkle flour over the mushrooms and onions and stir until fully incorporated. Then pour in the broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet.
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  15. Return the beef patties and any juices to the skillet. Simmer until the patties are cooked through, 2 to 3 minutes.
  16. Shave the parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Stir almost everything into the gravy (save some for garnishing).
  17. Divide the asparagus between four plates (or two plates and two lunch containers) and place the steak and gravy over top. Enjoy!
