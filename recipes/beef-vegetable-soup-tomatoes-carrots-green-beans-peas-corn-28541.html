Steps

  1. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  2. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    1 medium yellow onion
  3. Once the pot is hot, add onion and beef; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    1 ½ lb lean ground beef
  4. Add broth, frozen veggies, tomatoes, Worcestershire, and spices to the pot, then stir to combine the soup. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer until the veggies are soft, about 10 minutes. Remove from heat.
    64 fl oz (8 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    5 cups frozen mixed vegetables (carrots, corn, green beans, peas)
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    4 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    1 tsp garlic powder
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the soup and stir to combine. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  6. To serve, divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!
