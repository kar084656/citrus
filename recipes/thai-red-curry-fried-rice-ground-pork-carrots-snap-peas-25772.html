Ingredients

  • 1 cupjasmine rice
  • 1 ½ lblean ground pork
  • 1lime
  • 1 (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • ¼ cupThai curry paste, red
  • fish sauce
  • salt

Steps

  1. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup jasmine rice
    2 cup water
    ½ tsp salt
  2. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. While the rice is cooking, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add pork, carrots, curry paste, and salt. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    1 ½ lb lean ground pork
    1 (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ¼ cup Thai curry paste, red
    ½ tsp salt
  5. Meanwhile, wash and dry snap peas. Cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
  6. When the pork is done, add snap peas and water to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the peas are just tender and sauce has thickened slightly, 1-2 minutes. Cover and remove from heat.
    ⅔ cup water
  7. While the snap peas are cooking, wash, dry, and cut lime into wedges. Set aside for serving.
    1 lime
  8. When the rice is done, add to the skillet along with fish sauce. Set over high heat and cook, stirring constantly, until warmed through, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 tsp fish sauce
  9. To serve, divide fried rice between plates or bowls. Squeeze lime over top and enjoy!
