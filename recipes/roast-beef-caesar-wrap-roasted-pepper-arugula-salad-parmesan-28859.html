Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ cupCaesar dressing
  • 16 ozdeli roast beef
  • 1English cucumber
  • 4large flour tortillas
  • ⅔ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 1 (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers

Steps

  1. Wash and dry arugula (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Place in a medium bowl.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. Wash, dry, trim, and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the arugula.
    1 English cucumber
  3. Drain and roughly chop roasted peppers. Add to the bowl, along with cheese and dressing, then toss to combine the salad.
    1 (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    ⅔ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    ½ cup Caesar dressing
  4. Place tortillas on a flat surface and divide roast beef between them. Top with a portion of the salad and roll tightly to close. (Reserve remaining salad for serving.)
    4 large flour tortillas
    16 oz deli roast beef
  5. To serve, divide wraps and remaining salad between plates. Enjoy!
