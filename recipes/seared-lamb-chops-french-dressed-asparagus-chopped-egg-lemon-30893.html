Steps

  1. Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 small bunches asparagus
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 lemon
  3. Snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus.
  4. Once the water in the pot is boiling, add asparagus and salt; cook until tender-crisp, 2-3 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer asparagus to the colander, and run under cold water to cool; transfer to a plate and set aside. (Reserve boiling water in pot for the next step.)
    ½ tsp salt
  5. Carefully add eggs to the reserved pot, reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    4 eggs
  6. Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a small bowl.
    1 shallot
  7. Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the shallot. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  8. Add capers, oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper to the bowl. Stir to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tbsp capers
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  9. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. While the skillet heats up, pat the lamb chops dry with paper towels and place on another plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    8 lamb loin chops
    1 tsp salt
    1 tsp black pepper
  11. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until lamb is golden brown and cooked through, about 3 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate and set aside to rest.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. Meanwhile, cut lemon into wedges and set aside for serving.
  13. Peel and roughly chop the eggs. Season with salt.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  14. Divide lamb and asparagus between plates. Top asparagus with dressing, chopped egg, and reserved parsley. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing and enjoy!
