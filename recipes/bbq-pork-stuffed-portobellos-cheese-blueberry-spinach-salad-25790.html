Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • ¼ cuphoney mustard dressing
  • 1 ½ lblean ground pork
  • 8 capsportobello mushrooms
  • 1 cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • barbecue sauce
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    8 caps portobello mushrooms
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    1 (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  3. Remove stems from portobello mushrooms and carefully scrape out the gills. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, rub with oil, and season with spices on both sides; arrange cap-side up.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  4. Place mushrooms in the oven and broil until starting to soften, about 6 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add pork and cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    1 ½ lb lean ground pork
  7. When the pork is done, add barbecue sauce, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    ⅔ cup barbecue sauce
  8. Using tongs, carefully flip the mushrooms, cap-side down. Fill with BBQ pork and top with cheese.
    1 cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  9. Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to broil until the mushrooms are soft and cheese is melted, 4-5 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  10. Meanwhile, place spinach, blueberries, and dressing in a medium bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup honey mustard dressing
  11. To serve, divide stuffed mushrooms and salad between plates. Enjoy!
