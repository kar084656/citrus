Ingredients

  • 2 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 lbcremini mushrooms
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 8 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash, dry, and thinly slice mushrooms; transfer to a medium bowl and set aside.
    1 lb cremini mushrooms
  2. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Working in batches if necessary, add chicken in a single layer and cook until golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, and loosely cover with aluminum foil (the chicken will not be cooked all the way through).
    4 tsp butter, unsalted
  5. While the chicken browns, peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  6. Return the skillet to medium heat, add more butter, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and garlic to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until onion begins to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  7. Add mushrooms to the skillet and cook until golden brown and tender, 4-5 minutes.
  8. While the mushrooms cook, place peas in a colander and run under hot water (from the tap) to thaw.
    1 cup frozen peas
  9. Add peas, cream, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) heavy cream
    1 tsp rosemary
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  10. Return chicken to the skillet (overlapping the chicken thighs slightly, if necessary), along with any accumulated juices. Cover and simmer until chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes.
  11. Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  12. When the chicken is done, add Parmesan to the skillet and stir to combine.
  13. To serve, divide chicken and veggies between plates, spooning skillet sauce on top. Enjoy!
