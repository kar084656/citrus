Ingredients

  • 2 stickscelery
  • 4 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • 1 ½ lbnew potatoes
  • ½ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ cupsun-dried tomato pesto
  • 4 mediumzucchini squash
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • mayonnaise
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 ½ lb new potatoes
    4 medium zucchini squash
    2 sticks celery
  3. Halve potatoes. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with spices; stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring once, until potatoes are golden and fork-tender, about 25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. Meanwhile, trim and halve zucchini lengthwise. Scrape out the soft flesh from the middle of each half, place in a medium bowl, and set aside for later use.
  6. Arrange zucchini halves, cut-side up, on another baking sheet; drizzle with additional oil and season with salt and pepper.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. Add zucchini boats to the oven and bake until starting to soften, 5-7 minutes.
  8. Meanwhile, roughly chop reserved zucchini flesh and return to the bowl.
  9. Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the zucchini.
  10. Drain tuna and flake with a fork. Add to the bowl along with Parmesan, salt, and pepper. Set aside.
    4 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
    ½ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. Place pesto, mayo, salt, and pepper in a small bowl; stir to combine the sauce. Add half of the sauce to the bowl with the tuna and stir to combine the filling. (Set remaining sauce aside for serving.)
    ½ cup sun-dried tomato pesto
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  12. Remove zucchini boats from the oven and divide filling between them.
  13. Return zucchini boats to the oven and continue to bake until flesh is tender and filling is warmed through, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  14. To serve, divide zucchini boats, potatoes, and remaining sauce between plates. Enjoy!
