Ingredients

  • 2bananas
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblackberries
  • 4 cupsfrozen mixed berries
  • 320 mlgranola

  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • cinnamon, ground
  • honey

Steps

  1. This recipe will make about 6 cups of smoothie. Work in batches to best fit your blender.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 bananas
    1 lime
    1 (6 oz) pkg blackberries
  3. Peel and thinly slice bananas; set aside.
  4. Zest and juice lime into a blender.
  5. Add frozen mixed berries, yogurt, milk, honey, and cinnamon to the blender.
    4 cup frozen mixed berries
    2 cups plain Greek yogurt
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
    ¼ cup honey
    ½ tsp cinnamon, ground
  6. Blend on high speed until smooth, 1-2 minutes.
  7. To serve, divide smoothie between bowls and top with banana, blackberries, and granola. Enjoy!
    1 ⅓ cup granola
