Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 green bell peppers
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    4 tomatoes
    1 small bunch cilantro
  2. Drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans
  3. Peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel, trim, and small dice carrots. Place both in a medium bowl.
    1 medium yellow onion
  4. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  5. While the pot heats up, trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Add to the bowl with the other veggies.
  6. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    4 tsp virgin coconut oil
    ½ tsp salt
  7. Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Small dice tomatoes. Add both to the reserved bowl.
    4 cloves garlic
  8. Once the veggies are soft, add ginger, garlic, tomato, and spices to the pot. Cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    1 tbsp garam masala
    1 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
  9. Add beans, coconut milk, and water to the pot; bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until beans are tender and curry has thickened, about 10 minutes.
    1 (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ¼ cup water
  10. Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  11. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  12. Arrange naan on a baking sheet pan and place in the oven; broil, flipping once, until golden brown and toasted, 1-2 minutes.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  13. Remove naan from the oven, spread with oil, and sprinkle with half of the cilantro; cut into wedges. (Reserve remaining cilantro for the curry.)
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
  14. When the curry is done, remove from heat. Add reserved cilantro and stir to combine.
  15. Divide curry between bowls and serve with naan on the side. Enjoy!
