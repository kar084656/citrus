Ingredients

  • 90 mlblue cheese dressing
  • ½ cupdried cranberries
  • 2Gala apples
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
  • 1 ½ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 ½ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. Meanwhile, wash, dry, and chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  5. Wash, dry, quarter, and core apples; thinly slice. Add to the bowl with the lettuce along with cranberries. Set aside.
    2 Gala apples
    ½ cup dried cranberries
  6. Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  7. Just before serving, add dressing, salt, and pepper to the bowl, then toss to combine the salad.
    ⅓ cup blue cheese dressing
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. To serve, divide steak and salad between plates. Enjoy!
