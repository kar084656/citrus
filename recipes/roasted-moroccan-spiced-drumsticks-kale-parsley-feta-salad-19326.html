Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl, along with oil and spices, and whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 cloves garlic
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp cumin, ground
    1 ½ tsp coriander, ground
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  3. Add chicken to the bowl with the marinade and turn to coat. Transfer drumsticks to the baking sheet, spreading them out in a single layer.
    2 ½ lb chicken drumsticks
  4. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, turning once, until the chicken is golden brown and cooked through, 30-35 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 bunch kale
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 English cucumber
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  6. Juice lemon into a clean medium bowl. Add oil, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Finely chop leaves and add to the bowl with the dressing; using your hands, massage kale with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes.
  8. Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces; add to the bowl.
  10. Halve tomatoes; add to the bowl, along with feta, and toss to combine the salad.
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  11. To serve, divide chicken and salad between plates. Enjoy!
