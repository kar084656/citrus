Ingredients

  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • 1 ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1lemon
  • 2 (16 oz) pkgspotato gnocchi
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • crushed red pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 crowns broccoli
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    1 lemon
  3. Once the water in the pot is boiling, add gnocchi and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi begins to float to the surface, about 3 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.
    2 (16 oz) pkgs potato gnocchi
    1 tsp salt
  4. While the gnocchi are cooking, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Place both in a small bowl.
    1 medium yellow onion
    4 cloves garlic
  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces.
    1 ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, chicken, and spices. Cook, stirring frequently, until chicken is golden and cooked through, about 5 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  8. Meanwhile, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add broccoli and snap peas to the skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
  9. Zest and juice lemon into the skillet, then add cooked gnocchi and stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi is warmed through, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. To serve, divide gnocchi, veggies, and chicken between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
