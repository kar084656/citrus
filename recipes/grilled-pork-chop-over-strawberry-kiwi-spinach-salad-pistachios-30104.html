Grilled Pork Chop over Strawberry-Kiwi Spinach Salad with Pistachios

Ingredients

  • 2 (5 oz) pkgsbaby spinach
  • 4kiwifruit
  • ⅔ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • 4pork chops, boneless
  • 1shallot
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • black pepper
  • brown sugar
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • paprika
  • salt
  • sesame seeds
  • white wine vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. While the pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    4 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add pork chops and grill until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    4 kiwifruit
    2 (5 oz) pkgs baby spinach
  5. Peel and mince shallot. Place in a large bowl along with oil, vinegar, sesame seeds, Worcestershire, sugar, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 shallot
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp white wine vinegar
    2 tbsp sesame seeds
    4 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    4 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp paprika
  6. Trim and thinly slice strawberries. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. Peel, trim, and quarter kiwis lengthwise, then slice crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  8. Roughly chop pistachios. Add to the bowl, along with the spinach, and toss to combine the salad.
    ⅔ cup pistachios, unsalted
  9. Thinly slice pork chops.
  10. To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Top with sliced pork and enjoy!
