Pan-Fried Cajun Chicken Thighs with Zucchini & Carrot Rice Pilaf

Ingredients

  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 cupjasmine rice
  • 1 (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • butter, unsalted
  • Cajun seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    1 medium yellow onion
  3. Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, carrots, and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    4 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ½ tsp salt
  4. Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and small dice zucchini. Add to the pan and cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender, 3-4 minutes more.
    2 medium zucchini squash
  5. While the veggies are cooking, using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water.
    1 cup jasmine rice
  6. When the veggies are tender, add rice and broth to the pan, stir to combine, and bring to a boil over high heat.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  7. Once the liquid comes to a boil, cover the pan and reduce heat to low. Cook rice pilaf until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove from heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  8. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  9. While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels and place on a plate; sprinkle with Cajun seasoning on both sides.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  10. Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and cover with aluminum foil to keep warm.
    ¼ cup butter, unsalted
  11. To serve, divide rice pilaf and chicken between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
