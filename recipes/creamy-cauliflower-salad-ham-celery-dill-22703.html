Ingredients

  • 1 medium headcauliflower
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • 16 ozham steak, boneless
  • 1shallot
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • Dijon mustard
  • garlic powder
  • mayonnaise
  • paprika
  • red wine vinegar
  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium head cauliflower
    2 sticks celery
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
  3. Cut cauliflower into 1-inch florets and slice stems into smaller pieces.
  4. Once the water comes to a boil, uncover, then add cauliflower and salt; stir for a few seconds. Cook until tender-crisp, about 3 minutes. Drain and set aside to cool slightly.
    ½ tsp salt
  5. Meanwhile, place mayo, Dijon, vinegar, and spices in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    4 tsp Dijon mustard
    4 tsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp paprika
  6. Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 shallot
  7. Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl.
  8. Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. Small dice ham. Add to the bowl, along with the cauliflower, and stir to combine the salad.
    16 oz ham steak, boneless
  10. To serve, divide salad between bowls and enjoy!
