Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lemon
    2 yellow bell peppers
    2 oranges
    2 grapefruits
    1 avocado
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. Zest lemon into a medium saucepan (set lemon aside for later use).
  3. Add quinoa and broth to the saucepan with the zest, then bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    1 cup quinoa
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  4. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    2 (15 oz) cans cannellini (white kidney) beans
  5. Juice the lemon into a large bowl, then add oil, Dijon, maple syrup, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp Dijon mustard
    4 tsp pure maple syrup
    1 tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Peel and slice shallots into thin rings; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    2 shallots
  7. Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; add to the bowl.
  8. Cut off skin and white pith from the oranges and grapefruits. Slice between the membranes to release segments; cut into bite-sized pieces and add both to the bowl, along with the beans. Stir to combine and set aside.
  9. Transfer the cooked quinoa to a baking sheet pan. Spread out in an even layer and let cool for 5 minutes.
  10. Meanwhile, halve and pit avocado; scoop the flesh out with a spoon and medium dice. Add to the bowl with the dressing and citrus fruit.
  11. Add cooled quinoa to the bowl and gently stir to combine the salad.
  12. To serve, divide spinach between plates and top with salad. Enjoy!
