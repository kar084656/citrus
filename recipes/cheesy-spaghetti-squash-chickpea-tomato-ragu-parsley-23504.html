Ingredients

  • 2 (14.5 oz) cansdiced tomatoes
  • 2 (15 oz) cansgarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1 small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 4 ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 mediumspaghetti squash
  • 2 mediumyellow onions
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 500°F and place rack in the center position. Line a large baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. Wash, dry, and trim each squash, then halve lengthwise, and scrape out the seeds. Rub the inside of the squash with oil and season with salt and pepper. Arrange halves, cut-side down, on the baking sheet.
    2 medium spaghetti squash
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Place squash in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender, so a knife can easily pierce through the flesh, 20-25 minutes.
  4. While the squash is roasting, peel, halve, and medium dice onions. Place in a large bowl.
    2 medium yellow onions
  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, peel and thinly slice garlic. Add to the bowl with the onion.
    4 cloves garlic
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, crushed red pepper, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to brown, 6-8 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
    ½ tsp salt
  8. Meanwhile, drain and rinse chickpeas.
    2 (15 oz) cans garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  9. Once the onion is starting to brown, add chickpeas, tomatoes, and more salt to the skillet; stir to combine the ragù. Reduce heat to medium-low and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the squash is done.
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    ½ tsp salt
  10. Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Place in the reserved bowl and set aside.
    4 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. Remove squash from the oven and allow to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  12. While the squash is cooling, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add half of the parsley to skillet, stir to combine, and remove from heat. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  13. Once the squash has cooled slightly, scrape the inside with a fork to separate into spaghetti-like strands (use an oven mitt or towel to hold it with your other hand). Add squash to the bowl with the Parmesan and gently toss to combine.
  14. To serve, divide squash and veggie ragù between plates or bowls. Top with reserved parsley and enjoy!
