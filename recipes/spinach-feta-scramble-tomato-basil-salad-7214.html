Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 8eggs
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 4tomatoes
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry tomatoes; slice into wedges and place in a medium bowl with oil, salt, and pepper.
    4 tomatoes
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  2. Wash and dry the basil; pick the leaves off the stems and slice into ribbons. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes and toss gently to combine the salad; set aside.
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  3. Crack the eggs into a medium bowl and season with pepper; whisk together.
    8 eggs
    ½ tsp black pepper
  4. Preheat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  5. Add spinach and feta to eggs and stir gently to combine.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  6. Add oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet. Scramble the eggs using a combination of stirring and flipping, making sure to bring in all the mixture from the edges of the pan. Cook until the eggs are no longer runny.
  8. To serve, divide eggs and tomato salad between plates. Enjoy!
