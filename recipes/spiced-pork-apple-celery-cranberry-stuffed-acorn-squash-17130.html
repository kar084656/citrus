Ingredients

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 500°F and place rack in the center position. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 acorn squash
    2 Gala apples
    4 sticks celery
  3. Halve each acorn squash lengthwise, trim ends, then scoop out the seeds. Rub the inside of the squash with oil and season with salt and pepper; arrange halves, cut-side down, on the baking sheet.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. Place squash in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender, so a knife can easily pierce through the flesh, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. Meanwhile, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a small bowl.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  6. Quarter and core apples, then medium dice; add to the bowl with the onion and garlic.
  7. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. While the skillet heats up, trim and small dice celery; add to the bowl with the veggies and apple.
  9. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, apple, pork, and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 5-6 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ lb lean ground pork
    1 tsp salt
    1 tsp cinnamon, ground
    ½ tsp nutmeg, ground
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  10. When the pork is done, add broth, cranberries, and sugar; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 2-3 minutes more. Cover and remove from heat.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ cup dried cranberries
    4 tsp brown sugar
  11. To serve, divide squash halves between plates and fill with the pork and veggie stuffing. Enjoy!
