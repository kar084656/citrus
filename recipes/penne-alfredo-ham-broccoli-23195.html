Ingredients

  • 1 (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
  • 1 (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 16 ozham steak, boneless
  • 10 ozpenne pasta
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz penne pasta
  2. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, broccoli, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies start to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. Meanwhile, medium dice ham.
    16 oz ham steak, boneless
  6. When the veggies are soft, add ham and Alfredo sauce to the skillet. Stir to combine and continue to cook until heated through, 1-2 minutes more. Cover and remove from heat.
    1 (16 oz) jar Alfredo sauce
  7. When the pasta is done, drain in a colander. Add to the skillet with the sauce and stir to combine.
  8. To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and enjoy!
