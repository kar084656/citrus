Ingredients

  • ¼ cupalmonds
  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 pintsgrape tomatoes
  • 2lemons
  • ½ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • 10 ozpenne pasta
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until almost done, about 6 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz penne pasta
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    2 crowns broccoli
    2 lemons
    1 small pkg fresh basil
    2 pints grape tomatoes
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. Small dice mozzarella, place in a large bowl, and set aside.
    1 (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  4. Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
  5. When the pasta is almost done, add broccoli to the pot; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until pasta reaches desired firmness and broccoli is tender, 3-4 minutes more.
  6. Meanwhile, peel garlic and place in a blender.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. Zest and juice lemons into the blender with the garlic.
  8. Pick basil leaves off the stems; add to the blender along with nutritional yeast, almonds, water, oil, salt, and pepper. Blend on high until very smooth, 1-2 minutes. Add to the bowl with the mozzarella.
    ½ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    ¼ cup almonds
    ¼ cup water
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  9. When the pasta and broccoli are done, drain and rinse under cold water to cool; add to the bowl.
  10. Halve tomatoes; add to the bowl, along with mixed greens, and stir to combine the pasta salad.
  11. To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls and enjoy!
