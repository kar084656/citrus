Ingredients

  • 2anchovy fillets, packed in oil
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 8dried apricots
  • 12eggs
  • ½ cuppine nuts
  • 4red bell peppers
  • 1 bunchSwiss chard
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • red wine vinegar
  • salt
  • smoked paprika

Steps

  1. Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell peppers; slice lengthwise into thin strips.
    4 red bell peppers
  2. Preheat a large ovenproof skillet over medium heat.
  3. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add peppers and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and starting to brown, about 10 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  4. Meanwhile, crack eggs into a medium bowl, then add salt and pepper. Whisk to combine.
    12 eggs
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Add feta to the bowl with the egg. Stir to combine and set aside.
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  6. Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  7. Mince anchovy fillets. Place in another medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, salt, and crushed red pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 anchovy fillets, packed in oil
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  8. Wash and dry chard; fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips and thinly slice the stems, keeping them separate. Add leaves to the bowl with the dressing and set aside. Reserve stems for the next step.
    1 bunch Swiss chard
  9. When the peppers are tender, add chard stems and smoked paprika to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    2 tsp smoked paprika
  10. Add vinegar and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until absorbed, about 30 seconds.
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
  11. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, about 5 minutes.
  12. Place skillet in the oven and broil until top of the frittata is just set, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
  13. Meanwhile, thinly slice apricots. Add to the bowl with the Swiss chard, along with pine nuts, and toss to combine the salad.
    8 dried apricots
    ½ cup pine nuts
  14. Cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!
