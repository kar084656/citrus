Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 4 stickscelery
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • 4flatbreads or naan
  • 1 (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
  • butter, unsalted
  • garam masala
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  2. While the pot heats up, wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice celery crosswise.
    4 sticks celery
  3. Once the pot is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add celery and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    4 tsp butter, unsalted
  4. Add broth, marinara, garam masala, and salt to the pot; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until flavors blend, 8-10 minutes.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    4 tsp garam masala
    ½ tsp salt
  5. Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  6. Arrange naan on a large baking sheet pan; place in the oven and broil, flipping once, until golden brown and toasted, 1-2 minutes.
    4 flatbreads or naan
  7. While the naan is toasting, slice peppers into strips.
    1 (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  8. Remove naan from oven and top with sliced peppers and blue cheese.
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  9. When the soup is almost done, return naan to the oven and broil until cheese is melted and warmed through, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven and cut into quarters.
  10. When the soup is done, wash and dry the spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Add spinach to the pot in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Remove from heat.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  11. Divide soup between bowls and serve with naan on the side. Enjoy!
