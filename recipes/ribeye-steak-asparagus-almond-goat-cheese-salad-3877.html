Ingredients

  • ½ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 2 small bunchesasparagus
  • 1 (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 1lemon
  • 1 ½ lbribeye steak
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 small bunches asparagus
    1 lemon
  2. Snap or cut off and discard the woody ends of the asparagus; cut the spears into 2-inch pieces at an angle. Transfer to a medium bowl and set aside.
  3. Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  4. Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  5. While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 ½ lb ribeye steak
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add the almonds. Cook, stirring frequently to avoid burning, until fragrant and lightly toasted, 3 to 4 minutes. Once done, transfer to a small bowl.
    ½ cup almonds, sliced
  7. Once the almonds have been transferred, increase the heat to medium-high. Add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. Carefully place the steak in the skillet; flipping every 15 to 30 seconds, cook the steak until desired doneness (5 to 7 minutes for medium-rare). (Use a thermometer or cut into the steak to check the doneness.) Once done, transfer the steak to a plate and let it rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
  9. Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, uncover and season with salt. Add the asparagus and cook until bright green, 2 to 4 minutes (depending on the thickness of the spears). Drain in a colander and run under cold water to stop the cooking process. Set aside to drain further.
    2 tbsp salt
  10. Grate all of the zest from the lemon, then juice it. Add the zest and juice to the bowl that held the asparagus.
  11. To the lemon zest and juice, add olive oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk together.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  12. Add the asparagus to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
  13. Crumble goat cheese into a small bowl.
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
  14. To serve, divide the steak and asparagus between plates, then top the asparagus with the crumbled goat cheese and toasted almonds.
