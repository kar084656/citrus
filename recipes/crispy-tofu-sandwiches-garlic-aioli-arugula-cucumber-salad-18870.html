Crispy Tofu Sandwiches with Garlic Aioli & Arugula-Cucumber Salad

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Cut tofu into ½-inch thick planks along the short side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    2 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  3. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lemon
    1 English cucumber
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  4. Zest lemon into a small bowl (reserve lemon for the dressing). Add mayo and spices, then stir to combine the aioli.
    ½ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ½ tsp garlic powder
  5. Place tofu on the baking sheet. Spread 1 tablespoon aioli over top side of the tofu, sprinkle with half of the bread crumbs, and drizzle with half of the oil. Carefully flip tofu and repeat with 1 tablespoon aioli, remaining bread crumbs, and remaining oil. (Reserve remaining aioli for sandwiches.)
    1 cup panko bread crumbs
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. Place tofu in the oven and bake, turning once, until the breading is crispy and golden brown, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. Meanwhile, juice the lemon into a large bowl. Add oil, maple syrup, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. Trim and thinly slice cucumber crosswise into rounds; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. Add broccoli slaw and arugula to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  10. Toast buns if desired.
    4 whole grain buns or rolls
  11. Spread the remaining aioli over both halves of each bun. Layer each with a small portion of salad (reserving the rest for serving) and crispy tofu.
  12. To serve, divide sandwiches and reserved salad between plates. Enjoy!
