Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    4 red bell peppers
    1 medium head cauliflower
    2 jalapeño peppers
    1 small bunch cilantro
    1 lime
  3. Peel and medium dice potatoes; transfer to a large baking sheet pan.
  4. Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; add to the baking sheet with the potato.
  5. Cut the cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Separate into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; add to the baking sheet.
  6. Drizzle veggies with oil and season with salt and pepper; toss to coat, then spread out in an even layer. Add sausages to the baking sheet, nestling them into the veggies.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    4 pork sausages
  7. Place baking sheet in the oven and roast until veggies are tender and sausages are cooked through, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. Meanwhile, halve jalapeño peppers lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Place in a blender. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  9. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add cilantro to the blender with the jalapeño (reserving a small handful for garnish).
  10. Juice lime into the blender.
  11. Peel garlic and add to the blender.
    2 cloves garlic
  12. Add mayo, water, salt, and pepper to the blender; purée on high until the sauce is smooth. Set aside.
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    ¼ cup water
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. When the sausages are done, transfer to a cutting board and slice crosswise into 1-inch thick pieces.
  14. To serve, divide veggies and sausage between bowls; pour sauce over top and sprinkle with reserved cilantro. Enjoy!
