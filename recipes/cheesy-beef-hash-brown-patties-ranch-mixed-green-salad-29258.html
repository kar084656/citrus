Ingredients

  • 2 cupsfrozen shredded hash brown potatoes
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 ½ lblean ground beef
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • 1 cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ketchup

Steps

  1. Place hash browns in a colander; rinse under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Using your hands, squeeze as much water from the hash browns as you can.
    2 cups frozen shredded hash brown potatoes
  2. Place hash browns in a medium bowl along with beef, cheese, eggs, salt, and pepper. Mix with your hands to combine, then form into 12 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    1 ½ lb lean ground beef
    1 cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
    2 eggs
    1 tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Working in batches, add patties and cook, undisturbed, until golden underneath, about 5 minutes; flip patties and cook an additional 5-6 minutes, until cooked through. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. Meanwhile, wash, dry, and halve grape tomatoes. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  6. Wash and dry the mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the tomatoes, along with dressing, and toss to combine the salad.
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
  7. To serve, divide salad and patties between plates. Drizzle patties with ketchup and enjoy!
    ¼ cup ketchup
