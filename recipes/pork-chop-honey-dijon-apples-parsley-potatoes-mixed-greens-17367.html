Ingredients

  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 clovesgarlic

  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 ½ lb new potatoes
    2 Granny Smith apples
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. Halve potatoes, place in a medium saucepan, cover with hot water (from the tap), and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer until potatoes can be easily pierced with a fork, 10-12 minutes. Drain and return to the saucepan; cover to keep warm.
  3. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    4 pork chops, boneless
    1 tsp salt
    1 tsp black pepper
  5. Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    4 tsp butter, unsalted
  6. While the chops are cooking, peel, halve, and thinly slice onions into half-moons; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    2 medium yellow onions
    4 cloves garlic
  7. Quarter and core apples, then slice into ¼-inch thick wedges; add to the bowl with the onion and garlic.
  8. When the chops are done, return the skillet (with cooking juices) to medium-low heat. Add onion, garlic, and apple; cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion and apple are soft, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
  9. Meanwhile, place vinegar, mayo, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. Halve tomatoes lengthwise and add to the bowl with the dressing; set aside.
  11. When the onion and apple are soft, add broth, Dijon, and honey to the skillet; bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 tsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp honey
  12. Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  13. Return the saucepan with potatoes to low heat; add butter, parsley, and salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until butter and parsley coat the potatoes, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp salt
  14. Add mixed greens to bowl with dressing and tomatoes; toss to combine the salad.
  15. To serve, divide pork chops, apple sauté, potatoes, and salad between plates. Enjoy!
