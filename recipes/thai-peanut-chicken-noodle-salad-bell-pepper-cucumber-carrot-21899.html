Ingredients

  • 1English cucumber
  • 1lime
  • 160 mlpeanut sauce
  • 1 (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • 1 (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • brown sugar
  • crushed red pepper
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  • soy sauce

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    2 yellow bell peppers
    1 English cucumber
  3. Once the water is boiling, remove from heat and add the rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes, or according to package directions, then drain in a colander and rinse under cold water; set aside.
    10 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  4. While the noodles are soaking, juice lime into a large bowl. Add peanut sauce, soy sauce, sugar, and crushed red pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ⅔ cup peanut sauce
    2 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. Trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  7. Place chicken on a cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces and add to the bowl.
    1 (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  8. Add drained noodles and carrots to the bowl. Toss to combine the noodle salad.
    1 (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  9. To serve, divide noodle salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
