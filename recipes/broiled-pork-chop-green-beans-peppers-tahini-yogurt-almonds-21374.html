Ingredients

  • ½ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 1 lbgreen beans

  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 4pork chops, bone-in
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • tahini
  • white wine vinegar

Steps

  1. Preheat the broiler to high and position rack about 6 inches from the top.
  2. Place pork chops on a broiler pan and season both sides with salt and pepper.
    4 pork chops, bone-in
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Place pan in the oven. Broil, flipping halfway through, until chops are no longer pink in the center and are browned at the edges, 12-14 minutes. Remove from oven and let rest for 5 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. While the skillet heats up, place yogurt, tahini, vinegar, and salt in a small bowl. Stir to combine and set aside.
    1 cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ cup tahini
    2 tbsp white wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add almonds and cook, stirring frequently, until golden and toasted, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate and set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    ½ cup almonds, sliced
  7. Wash, dry, and trim green beans; place in a medium bowl.
    1 lb green beans
  8. Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell peppers, then slice lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the green beans.
    2 orange bell peppers
  9. Return the skillet to high heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until blistered and tender, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. To serve, divide chops and tahini yogurt between plates; top yogurt with veggies and almonds. Enjoy!
