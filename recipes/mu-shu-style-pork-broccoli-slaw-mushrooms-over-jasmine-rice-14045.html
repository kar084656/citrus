Steps

  1. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup jasmine rice
    2 cup water
  2. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, wash, dry, and thinly slice mushrooms; transfer to a medium bowl and set aside.
    1 lb cremini mushrooms
  4. Pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board; thinly slice crosswise into bite-sized strips and season with salt and pepper.
    4 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork strips and stir-fry until browned and cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  7. Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic; place in a small bowl along with soy sauce, cornstarch, peanut butter, chili-garlic sauce, oil, vinegar, honey, and spices. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    4 cloves garlic
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    2 tbsp cornstarch
    4 tsp natural peanut butter
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp clove, ground
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. When the pork is done, use a slotted spoon to transfer the strips to a plate and set aside.
  9. Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until they start to soften, about 3 minutes.
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. Add broccoli slaw and sauce to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies are tender-crisp and sauce thickens, 2-3 minutes.
    2 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  11. Return pork to the skillet, along with any accumulated juices; stir to combine and cook until heated through, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  12. To serve, divide rice and pork stir-fry between plates. Enjoy!
