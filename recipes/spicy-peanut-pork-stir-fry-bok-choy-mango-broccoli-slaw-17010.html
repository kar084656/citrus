Steps

  1. Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  2. Add soy sauce, oil, and crushed red pepper to the bowl with the garlic; whisk to combine the marinade.
    4 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  3. Slice pork chops crosswise into ½-inch thick strips, add to the bowl with the marinade, toss to coat, and set aside.
    4 pork chops, boneless
  4. While the pork marinates, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    1 lime
    6 baby bok choy
    2 mangoes
  5. Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince ginger; place in a small bowl.
  6. Juice lime into the bowl with the ginger; add peanut butter, fish sauce, water, honey, and spices. Whisk to combine the peanut sauce and set aside.
    ¼ cup natural peanut butter
    2 tbsp fish sauce
    2 tbsp water
    4 tsp honey
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
  7. Place oil, soy sauce, vinegar, honey, and chili-garlic sauce in a large bowl; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    4 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp honey
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  8. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork strips and marinade; stir-fry until browned and cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. Meanwhile, trim and thinly slice bok choy crosswise into shred-like strips; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. Peel mangoes and slice flesh away from the pits, starting with the flat sides; large dice and add to the bowl.
  12. Add broccoli slaw to the bowl and toss to combine.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  13. When the pork is done, add peanut sauce, stir to combine, and cook until sauce is warmed through and pork is coated, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  14. To serve, divide pork stir-fry and slaw between plates. Sprinkle pork with additional crushed red pepper and enjoy!
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
