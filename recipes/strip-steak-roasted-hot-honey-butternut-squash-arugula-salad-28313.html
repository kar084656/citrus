Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1 smallbutternut squash
  • 1lemon
  • 1shallot
  • 1 ½ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • honey
  • hot sauce
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 small butternut squash
    1 lemon
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. Trim and peel squash; halve lengthwise, scrape out the seeds, and medium dice the flesh. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  4. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until squash is golden and fork-tender, about 20 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt on both sides.
    1 ½ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    1 tsp salt
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, about 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. When the squash is done, remove from the oven, then drizzle with honey and hot sauce. Stir to coat and set aside to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
    3 tbsp honey
    4 tsp hot sauce
  9. Meanwhile, zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  10. Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the lemon along with oil, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 shallot
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. Add arugula and cooled squash to the bowl with the dressing, then toss to combine the salad.
  12. Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. To serve, divide steak and salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
