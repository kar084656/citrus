Ingredients

Steps

  1. In a medium saucepan, combine quinoa, water, and salt; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    1 cup quinoa
    2 cup water
    ½ tsp salt
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 lemons
    1 (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 English cucumber
    2 peaches
    1 small pkg fresh mint
  3. Zest and juice lemons into a large bowl.
  4. Peel and mince shallots. Add to the bowl with the lemon along with oil, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 shallots
    ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Trim and slice strawberries lengthwise into ¼-inch thick pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. Trim and medium dice cucumber. Add to the bowl.
  7. Quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Medium dice the flesh and add to the bowl.
  8. Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. Roughly chop pistachios and add to the bowl.
    ⅔ cup pistachios, unsalted
  10. Crumble goat cheese into the bowl and set aside.
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
  11. When the quinoa is done, transfer to a large baking sheet pan. Spread out in an even layer and let cool for 5 minutes. Once cool, add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  12. To serve, divide the salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
