Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 green bell peppers
    2 red bell peppers
    2 sticks celery
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  4. While the pot heats up, small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the bell peppers and set aside.
  5. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add turkey and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ lb ground turkey
    4 tsp chili powder
    1 ½ tsp garlic powder
    1 ½ tsp oregano, dried
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    2 (15 oz) cans cannellini (white kidney) beans
  7. When the turkey is done, add veggies to the pot; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  8. Add beans, broth, tomatoes, and hot sauce to the pot; stir to combine the soup, then bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until beans are tender, about 10 minutes. Remove from heat.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    4 tsp hot sauce
  9. While the soup is cooking, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  10. While the skillet heats up, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in the reserved bowl.
  11. Once the skillet is hot, add bread crumbs and cook, stirring frequently, until golden brown, 3-4 minutes. Transfer toasted bread crumbs to the bowl with the parsley and stir to combine.
    ⅔ cup panko bread crumbs
  12. To serve, divide soup between bowls and top with parsley bread crumbs. Enjoy!
