Steps

  1. Cut the tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    2 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    1 small bunch cilantro
    1 lime
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. While the skillet heats up, peel and small dice onion.
    1 medium red onion
  5. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the onion and cook, stirring often, until slightly softened, about 5 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. Meanwhile, peel and coarsely grate carrots. Trim and mince celery. Add both to the skillet and stir to combine.
  7. Peel and mince garlic; add to the skillet.
    4 cloves garlic
  8. Crumble the tofu with your fingers into the skillet. Add soy sauce and spices; increase heat to medium-high and continue to cook, stirring often, until veggies are soft and tofu begins to brown, about 6 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    1 ½ tsp turmeric
    1 ½ tsp curry powder
    1 ½ tsp cumin
    1 ½ tsp paprika
  9. Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  10. Juice lime into the skillet; add cilantro and stir to combine.
  11. Place tortillas on a flat surface, spread with mayo and top with tofu scramble. Tightly roll to close and cut in half. Enjoy!
    4 large flour tortillas
    4 tsp mayonnaise
