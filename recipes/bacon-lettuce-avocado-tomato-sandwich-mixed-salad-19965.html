Ingredients

  • 1avocado
  • 8 slicesbacon

  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1lemon

  • 2tomatoes

  • honey
  • mayonnaise
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    2 medium carrots
    1 head romaine lettuce
    2 tomatoes
    1 avocado
  2. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, turning occasionally, until crispy, 6-8 minutes. Using tongs, transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate.
    8 slices bacon
  4. Meanwhile, juice lemon into a medium bowl; add oil, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. Separate lettuce leaves; set aside 1 leaf per sandwich and chop or tear remaining leaves into bite-sized pieces. Add chopped lettuce to the bowl, along with broccoli slaw, and toss to combine the salad; set aside.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  7. Slice tomatoes into thin rounds.
  8. Halve and pit avocado; cut crosswise while still in the skin, then scoop out with a spoon.
  9. Toast bread if desired.
    8 slices whole grain bread
  10. Spread mayo over each bread slice; layer half of the slices with lettuce, tomato, avocado, and bacon. Place remaining slices on top to close sandwiches.
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
  11. To serve, divide salad and sandwiches between plates. Enjoy!
