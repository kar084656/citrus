Maple-Dijon Chicken Drumsticks with Lemony Dill, Kale & Cabbage Slaw

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ lbchicken drumsticks
  • 1 small bunchfresh dill
  • 1 bunchkale
  • 2lemons
  • 1 medium headnapa cabbage
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • pure maple syrup
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Place oil, maple syrup, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a large baking dish; whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup
    4 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Pat chicken dry with paper towels, add to the baking dish with the marinade, and turn to coat.
    2 ½ lb chicken drumsticks
  4. Place chicken in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning halfway through, until chicken is cooked through, 25-30 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 lemons
    1 bunch kale
    1 medium head napa cabbage
    1 small bunch fresh dill
  6. Zest and juice lemons into a large bowl. Add oil, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. Wash, dry, and fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. Trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage; halve the cabbage lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise. Add to the bowl. Using your hands, massage the greens with the dressing until wilted, about 2 minutes.
  9. Shave the dill leaves off the stems; discard the stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw.
  10. To serve, divide chicken and slaw between plates. Enjoy!
