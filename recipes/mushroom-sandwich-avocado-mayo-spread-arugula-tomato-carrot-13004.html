Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    4 caps portobello mushrooms
    1 avocado
    2 tomatoes
    2 medium carrots
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. Place oil, soy sauce, vinegar, maple syrup, paprika, and pepper in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp soy sauce
    4 tsp balsamic vinegar
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. Remove mushroom stems and carefully scrape out the gills. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with marinade, and turn to coat. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  5. Place mushrooms in the oven and roast until tender, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside.
  6. Meanwhile, halve and pit the avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out into the reserved bowl.
  7. Add nutritional yeast, mayo, and spices to the bowl with the avocado; mash with a potato masher or fork until smooth. Set avocado mayo aside.
    ½ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. Slice tomatoes. Peel, trim, and coarsely grate the carrots. Transfer both to a plate and set aside.
  9. Toast bread if desired.
    8 slices whole grain bread
  10. Slice mushrooms into strips.
  11. Place bread slices on a flat surface and spread evenly with avocado mayo. Top half the slices with mushrooms, tomato, carrot, and arugula. Cover sandwiches with remaining bread slices.
  12. To serve, divide sandwiches between plates and enjoy!
