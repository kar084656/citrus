Ingredients

  • 2 (15 oz) canspinto beans
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
  • 1 cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • 12small flour tortillas
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • barbecue sauce
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • onion powder
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    2 hearts romaine hearts
    2 yellow bell peppers
  2. Trim and small dice zucchini. Set aside.
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. While the skillet heats up, drain and rinse the beans in a colander.
    2 (15 oz) cans pinto beans
  5. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add zucchini, beans, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Meanwhile, halve lettuce lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Place in a medium bowl.
  7. Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Add to the bowl with the lettuce, along with dressing, and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
  8. Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    12 small flour tortillas
  9. When the zucchini and beans are done, add barbecue sauce to the skillet and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
    ½ cup barbecue sauce
  10. Divide tortillas between plates and fill with bean mixture, cheese, and a small portion of the salad. Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!
    1 cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
