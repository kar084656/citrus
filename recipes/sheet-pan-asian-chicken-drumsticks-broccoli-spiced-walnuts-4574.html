Ingredients

  • 2 ½ lbchicken drumsticks
  • 4 clovesgarlic

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F, position a rack in the center position and another in the bottom position. Line two large baking sheet pans with aluminum foil (optional).
  2. Peel and mince garlic and ginger; transfer to a large bowl.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    4 cloves garlic
  3. Add soy sauce, oil, chili-garlic sauce, and sugar to the bowl and whisk to combine the marinade. Add the chicken and toss to coat.
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
    4 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp brown sugar
    2 ½ lb chicken drumsticks
  4. Spread chicken out in a single layer on a prepared sheet pan. Place on the bottom rack of the oven and roast until the chicken is cooked through, 30-35 minutes. Reserve marinade.
  5. Wash broccoli and cut into small, bite-sized florets. Thinly slice the thicker stem. Transfer to a medium bowl.
    2 crowns broccoli
  6. Toss broccoli with soy sauce and oil. Spread out in a single layer on the other baking sheet and place in the oven for 5-8 minutes while you prepare the nuts.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
  7. Remove chicken from the oven. Use tongs to flip each drumstick. Spoon on remaining marinade, and return to the oven.
  8. Roughly chop walnuts. In a small bowl, toss walnuts with water to moisten.
    ⅔ cup walnuts
    1 tbsp water
  9. In another small bowl, stir together sugar, cinnamon, and cayenne pepper. Toss moistened walnuts in the spice mixture.
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  10. Remove the broccoli pan from the oven and sprinkle the walnuts on top. Return the pan to the oven for an additional 5-8 minutes or until broccoli is tender-crisp, and walnuts are toasted.
  11. To serve, place chicken, broccoli, and walnuts on a plate. Enjoy!
