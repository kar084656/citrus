Ingredients

  • 2 (15 oz) canscannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 1 medium headcauliflower
  • 48 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 6 clovesgarlic
  • 2 pintsgrape tomatoes
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • balsamic vinegar
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium head cauliflower
    2 pints grape tomatoes
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  2. Drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    2 (15 oz) cans cannellini (white kidney) beans
  3. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  4. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and medium dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    4 cloves garlic
  5. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add onion, garlic, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 4 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Italian seasoning
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Meanwhile, separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add cauliflower and water to the pot with the onion; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, while preparing the tomatoes.
    ¼ cup water
  7. Halve tomatoes; add to the pot along with the beans and broth. Stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer, stirring occasionally, until veggies are fork-tender, about 10 minutes.
    48 fl oz (6 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  8. Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  9. Peel and mince additional garlic; place in a small bowl along with oil and spices. Stir to combine the garlic oil.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. Place flatbread on a baking sheet pan and spread garlic oil over top; place in the oven and broil until golden and crisp, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven and slice crosswise into sticks.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  11. While the garlic bread is toasting, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  12. When the soup is done, add vinegar, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  13. Divide soup between bowls and top with basil. Serve with breadsticks on the side and enjoy!
