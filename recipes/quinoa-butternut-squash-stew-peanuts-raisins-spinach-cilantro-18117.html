Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 small butternut squash
    1 red bell pepper
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    1 small bunch cilantro
    1 lime
  2. Trim and peel squash; halve lengthwise, scrape out seeds, and small dice. Place in a small bowl.
  3. Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the squash and set aside.
  4. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  5. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  6. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and garlic; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 2 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. Add squash, bell pepper, tomatoes, water, quinoa, and spices to the pot. Stir to combine the stew and bring to a boil over high heat; once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover, and cook until squash and quinoa are tender, about 20 minutes. (Reserve bowl for next step.)
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    3 cup water
    1 cup quinoa
    2 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp salt
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  8. Meanwhile, roughly chop spinach and place in the reserved bowl.
  9. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  10. When the quinoa is tender, add spinach to the pot in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  11. Add peanut butter, additional water, raisins, and half of the cilantro to the stew. Stir to combine and continue to cook until warmed through, about 3 minutes. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
    ½ cup natural peanut butter
    ½ cup water
    ¼ cup raisins
  12. Meanwhile, roughly chop peanuts. Slice lime into wedges.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  13. Divide stew between bowls; top with reserved cilantro and peanuts. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing over and enjoy!
