Ingredients

  • 2 (6 oz) jarsartichoke hearts, marinated
  • 2 tbspcapers
  • 1English cucumber
  • ½ cupFrench vinaigrette
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 1lemon
  • ¼ cuppine nuts
  • 1 (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
  • 10 ozrotini pasta
  • 2 (7.5 oz) canswild pink salmon, traditional style
  • black pepper
  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz rotini pasta
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 small pkg fresh basil
    1 English cucumber
  3. Juice lemon into a large bowl.
  4. Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the bowl with the lemon juice.
  5. Drain and mince capers. Add to the bowl along with vinaigrette, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp capers
    ½ cup French vinaigrette
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. Trim and medium dice cucumber. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. Drain and roughly chop roasted peppers and artichoke hearts. Add both to the bowl.
    1 (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    2 (6 oz) jars artichoke hearts, marinated
  8. Drain salmon, add to the bowl, and flake with a fork.
    2 (7.5 oz) cans wild pink salmon, traditional style
  9. When the pasta is done, drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the pasta salad.
  10. To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls; sprinkle with pine nuts. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup pine nuts
