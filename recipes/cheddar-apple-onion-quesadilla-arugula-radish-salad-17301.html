Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    2 Granny Smith apples
    1 bunch radish
    1 avocado
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half-moons.
    1 medium yellow onion
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until onions are soft and golden brown, 6-7 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. Meanwhile, quarter and core apples, then thinly slice.
  6. Coarsely grate cheese.
    1 (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  7. Place tortillas on a flat surface and divide onion, apple, and cheese between them, arranging the filling on one half of each tortilla; fold in half to close.
    4 large flour tortillas
  8. Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels and return to medium heat.
  9. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Working in batches, add quesadillas and cook until cheese is melted and tortillas are golden, 2½-3 minutes per side. Remove to a plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a large bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp white wine vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. Trim and thinly slice radishes; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl, along with arugula, and toss to combine the salad.
  13. Cut each quesadilla into wedges and divide between plates. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!
