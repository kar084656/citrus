Ingredients

  • 4 mediumcarrots
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4pork sausages
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • 1 cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash

  • brown sugar

  • extra virgin olive oil
  • onion powder
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 medium carrots
    2 yellow bell peppers
    2 medium zucchini squash
  3. Peel, trim, and cut carrots crosswise into thin rounds; transfer to a large baking sheet pan.
  4. Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; add to the baking sheet with the carrot.
  5. Trim and slice zucchini crosswise into thin rounds; add to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil, then season with salt and pepper; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. Add sausages to the baking sheet, nestling them into the veggies.
    4 pork sausages
  7. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until veggies are fork-tender and sausages are cooked through, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. Meanwhile, in a medium saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes; fluff with a fork right before serving.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 cup whole wheat couscous
  9. While the couscous is cooking, place tomato sauce, Dijon, vinegar, Worcestershire, sugar, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the BBQ sauce.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    2 tbsp Dijon mustard
    4 tsp apple cider vinegar
    4 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    4 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp salt
    1 tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp chili powder
  10. When the sausages and veggies are done, transfer sausages to a cutting board and slice crosswise into thin rounds.
  11. Return sausages to the baking sheet with the veggies; drizzle with BBQ sauce, toss to combine, and spread out in an even layer.
  12. Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to roast until veggies and sauce are caramelized, 4-5 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  13. To serve, divide sausage, veggies, and couscous between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
