Steps

  1. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 small head red cabbage
    2 Granny Smith apples
    2 yellow bell peppers
  3. Once the pan is hot, coat with oil; add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 pork sausages
  4. While the sausages are cooking, place ketchup, water, Worcestershire, vinegar, and spices in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    ¼ cup ketchup
    2 tbsp water
    2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp curry powder
    1 tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  5. Juice lemon into a large bowl; add yogurt, mayo, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    4 tsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. Quarter cabbage lengthwise, then cut out and discard the core; slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. Quarter, core, and small dice apples; add to the bowl.
  8. Trim, halve, seed, and slice bell peppers into thin strips; add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw. Set aside.
  9. When the sausages are done, transfer to a cutting board and slice crosswise into thin rounds.
  10. Warm tortillas in a skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    4 large flour tortillas
  11. Place tortillas on a flat surface and top each with a small portion of slaw; arrange sausage slices on top and drizzle with sauce. Roll tightly to close. (Reserve remaining slaw for serving.)
  12. Cut wraps in half and divide between plates; serve with remaining slaw on the side. Enjoy!
