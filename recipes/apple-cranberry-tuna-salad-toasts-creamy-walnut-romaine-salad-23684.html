Ingredients

  • 4 stickscelery
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • ½ cupdried cranberries
  • 2Granny Smith apples
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
  • ½ cupwalnuts
  • 8 sliceswhole grain bread
  • mayonnaise
  • onion powder
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 sticks celery
    2 Granny Smith apples
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  2. Place yogurt, mayo, onion powder, and salt in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the dressing.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp salt
  3. Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. Quarter, core, and small dice apples. Add to the bowl along with the cranberries.
    ¼ cup dried cranberries
  5. Drain tuna and flake into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the tuna salad, and set aside.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  6. Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  7. Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Place in another medium bowl.
  8. Roughly chop walnuts. Add to the bowl with the lettuce along with additional cranberries, dressing, and salt. Toss to combine the salad and set aside.
    ½ cup walnuts
    ¼ cup dried cranberries
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
    ⅛ tsp salt
  9. Arrange bread on a large baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    8 slices whole grain bread
  10. Divide toast between plates and top with tuna salad. Serve with romaine salad on the side and enjoy!
