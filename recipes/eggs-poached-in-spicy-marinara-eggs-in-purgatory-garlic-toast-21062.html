Eggs Poached in Spicy Marinara (Eggs in Purgatory) with Garlic Toast

Ingredients

  • 8eggs
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (24 oz) jarsmarinara sauce
  • ⅔ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 4whole grain buns or rolls
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • crushed red pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell peppers; medium dice.
    2 yellow bell peppers
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic. Place half in a small bowl and set aside for the garlic toast.
    4 cloves garlic
  5. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper, remaining garlic, and crushed red pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, about 3 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. When the pepper is soft, increase heat to medium-high and add marinara to the skillet. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer.
    2 (24 oz) jars marinara sauce
  7. Meanwhile, wash, dry, and pick basil leaves off the stems; roughly chop.
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  8. Once the sauce is simmering, add basil and stir to combine. Using a stirring spoon, make a well for each egg in the marinara; crack an egg into each well. Cover and cook until the egg whites are firm but yolks are still soft, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    8 eggs
  9. While the eggs are cooking, add oil and spices to the bowl with the remaining garlic. Stir to combine the garlic oil.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  10. Split rolls in half and arrange on a large baking sheet pan; drizzle with the garlic oil.
    4 whole grain buns or rolls
  11. Place baking sheet in the oven and broil until rolls are toasted, 1-2 minutes; remove from oven.
  12. Divide eggs and sauce between plates or bowls and sprinkle with Parmesan. Serve with garlic toast on the side. Enjoy!
    ⅔ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
