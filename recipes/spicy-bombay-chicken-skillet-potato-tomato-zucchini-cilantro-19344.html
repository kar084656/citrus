Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 ½ lb new potatoes
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 orange bell peppers
    2 medium zucchini squash
    2 jalapeño peppers
    1 small bunch cilantro
  2. Halve potatoes, place in a large pot, and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, and simmer until potatoes are fork-tender, 10-15 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger and garlic; place both in a large bowl.
    4 cloves garlic
  4. Peel, halve, and medium dice onion; add to the bowl with the ginger and garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
  5. Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; add to the bowl.
  6. Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons; add to the bowl.
  7. Halve jalapeño peppers lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Small dice and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  8. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  9. While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Cut into bite-sized pieces.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  10. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and chicken; cook, stirring occasionally, until chicken is golden brown and almost cooked through, 6-7 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  12. When the chicken is done, add half of the cilantro (reserving the rest for garnish), tomatoes, pine nuts, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens, 3-4 minutes more.
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    ¼ cup pine nuts
    1 tsp coriander, ground
    1 tsp turmeric, ground
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  13. When the potatoes are done, drain and add to the skillet; stir to combine and remove from heat.
  14. To serve, divide the chicken skillet between plates or bowls and top with reserved cilantro. Enjoy!
