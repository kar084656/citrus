Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Meanwhile, remove husks and silks from corn; set aside.
    4 ears corn
  3. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. While the grill pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season both sides with salt and pepper, then coat with BBQ sauce.
    4 pork chops, bone-in
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    3 tbsp barbecue sauce
  5. Once the grill pan is hot, coat with butter; working in batches if necessary, add pork chops and grill until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and coat with additional BBQ sauce.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ cup barbecue sauce
  6. Once the water is boiling, uncover the pot and reduce heat to medium; use tongs to lower the corn into the water and cook until just tender, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
  7. Meanwhile, wash and dry parsley, then shave leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl along with dressing and mayo; whisk to combine.
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    3 tbsp honey mustard dressing
    4 tsp mayonnaise
  8. Add broccoli slaw to the bowl with dressing and stir to combine.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  9. To serve, divide pork chops, corn, and slaw between plates; spread corn with butter and sprinkle with salt. Enjoy!
    4 tsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp salt
