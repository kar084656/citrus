Ingredients

  • 2 medium headscauliflower
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • 2 (15 oz) cansgarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 4tomatoes
  • 1 ⅓ cupswhole wheat couscous
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil

Steps

  1. Wash cauliflower and tomatoes. Cut the cauliflower into bite-sized florets. Medium dice the tomatoes.
    2 medium heads cauliflower
    4 tomatoes
  2. Heat a sauté pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Peel and mince onion and garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    4 cloves garlic
  4. Coat bottom of pan with oil. Add onion and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  5. Drain and rinse chickpeas.
    2 (15 oz) cans garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  6. Add garlic and curry powder to pan and stir with onion until fragrant, 15-30 seconds.
    2 tbsp curry powder
  7. Add cauliflower and chickpeas and stir until heated through, 1-2 minutes.
  8. Add tomatoes, coconut milk, salt, and pepper; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cover. Cook until cauliflower is tender and liquid thickens, about 10 minutes.
    1 (13.5 oz) can coconut milk
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. In a small saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Remove from heat, add couscous, and stir. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 ⅓ cups whole wheat couscous
  10. Wash and dry cilantro. Shave the leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Stir ½ into the curry (save the rest for garnishing).
    1 small bunch cilantro
  11. Uncover couscous and fluff with a fork.
  12. To serve, place couscous in a bowl, top with curry and garnish with remaining cilantro. Enjoy!
