Ingredients

  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 64 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 lbcremini mushrooms
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 ½ lblean ground pork
  • 1 medium headnapa cabbage
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • black pepper
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • coriander, ground
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • soy sauce

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    2 medium carrots
    1 lb cremini mushrooms
    1 medium head napa cabbage
  2. Medium dice potatoes. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. Peel, trim, and small dice carrots. Add to the bowl with the potatoes.
  4. Thinly slice mushrooms. Add to the bowl.
  5. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  6. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl and set aside.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  7. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ lb lean ground pork
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
  8. Add veggies to the pot. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly softened, 3-4 minutes more.
  9. Meanwhile, trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into 1-inch wide strips.
  10. When the veggies are done, add cabbage, broth, soy sauce, and chili-garlic sauce to the pot; stir to combine the soup, then bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    64 fl oz (8 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  11. To serve, divide soup between bowls and enjoy!
