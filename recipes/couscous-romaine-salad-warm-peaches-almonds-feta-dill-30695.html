Couscous-Romaine Salad with Warm Peaches, Almonds, Feta & Dill

Ingredients

Steps

  1. In a medium saucepan, bring water and salt to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes; fluff with a fork.
    2 cup water
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 cup whole wheat couscous
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 lemons
    1 English cucumber
    2 heads romaine lettuce
    1 small bunch fresh dill
    4 peaches
  3. Juice lemons into a large bowl.
  4. Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the lemon juice along with oil, Dijon, sugar, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp Dijon mustard
    4 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. Trim and small dice cucumber. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. Halve lettuce lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl.
  7. Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl and set the salad aside. (Reserve remaining dill for serving.)
  8. When the couscous is done, transfer to a baking sheet pan. Spread out in an even layer and let cool slightly.
  9. Quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Slice each quarter into ½-inch thick wedges.
  10. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  11. While the skillet heats up, roughly chop almonds.
    ⅔ cup almonds
  12. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add peaches, almonds, sugar, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until peaches are golden brown and fork-tender, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. Add cooled couscous to the bowl with the salad and gently toss to combine.
  14. To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with warm peaches, almonds, feta, and reserved dill. Enjoy!
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
