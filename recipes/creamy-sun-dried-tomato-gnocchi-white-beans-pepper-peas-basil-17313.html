Ingredients

  • 16 fl ozalmond milk, unsweetened
  • 2 (15 oz) canscannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 2 small pkgsfresh basil
  • 2 cupsfrozen peas
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • ¼ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • 2 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • 8sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • cornstarch
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    2 (15 oz) cans cannellini (white kidney) beans
  2. Wash and dry bell peppers, then trim, seed, and small dice. Place in a small bowl.
    2 yellow bell peppers
  3. Peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the bell pepper.
    1 medium yellow onion
    4 cloves garlic
  4. Mince sun-dried tomatoes and add to the bowl.
    8 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  5. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  6. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies to the pot; cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for next step.)
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. Meanwhile, place water and cornstarch in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the slurry.
    2 tbsp water
    4 tsp cornstarch
  8. When the veggies are soft, add cornstarch slurry, almond milk, nutritional yeast, salt, and pepper to the pot; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens, 3-4 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) almond milk, unsweetened
    ¼ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  9. While the sauce simmers, wash and dry basil. Pick leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
    2 small pkgs fresh basil
  10. Add beans, gnocchi, peas, and half of the basil to the pot (reserve remaining basil for garnish). Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 4-5 minutes more.
    2 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    2 cups frozen peas
  11. To serve, divide creamy gnocchi between bowls. Top with remaining basil and enjoy!
