Ingredients

  • 4 mediumcarrots
  • 48 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes

  • 1 mediumred onion
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • curry powder
  • red lentils, dried
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 medium carrots
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 small bunch cilantro
  2. Peel and small dice onion; transfer to a medium bowl.
    1 medium red onion
  3. Peel, trim, and small dice carrots; add to the bowl with the onion.
  4. Trim and small dice zucchini; add to the bowl.
  5. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  6. While the pot heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    4 cloves garlic
  7. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, garlic, curry powder, and salt to the pot; cook, stirring often, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    3 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    3 tbsp curry powder
    2 tsp salt
  8. Add broth, coconut milk, tomatoes, and lentils to the pot; stir to combine, cover, and bring to a boil.
    48 fl oz (6 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    1 ½ cup red lentils, dried
  9. Once the soup is boiling, reduce heat to medium-low; simmer, stirring occasionally, until lentils and veggies are tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  11. When the soup is done, add cilantro and stir to combine.
  12. To serve, divide soup between bowls, top with yogurt, and enjoy!
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
