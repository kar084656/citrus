Ingredients

  • 2 small bunchesasparagus
  • 2 cupsfrozen corn
  • 2 (12 oz) bagsfrozen, riced cauliflower
  • 16 ozham steak, boneless
  • 1 small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 320 mlsalsa
  • 1 ⅓ cupsshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • Cajun seasoning
  • extra virgin olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash, dry, and snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears into bite-sized pieces. Transfer to a large baking sheet pan.
    2 small bunches asparagus
  3. Add riced cauliflower and corn to the baking sheet with the asparagus; drizzle with oil and sprinkle with Cajun seasoning. Stir to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 (12 oz) bags frozen, riced cauliflower
    2 cups frozen corn
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp Cajun seasoning
  4. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until veggies are starting to soften, about 12 minutes.
  5. When the veggies are soft, remove from the oven and stir. Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to roast until veggies are tender and golden, 8-10 minutes more.
  6. Meanwhile, preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. While the pan heats up, cut ham into portions. Sprinkle both sides with additional Cajun seasoning.
    16 oz ham steak, boneless
    2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  8. Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Working in batches if necessary, add ham steaks and grill, flipping once, until charred and warmed through, about 5 minutes. Remove to a plate and set aside.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. While the ham is cooking, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  10. When the veggies are done, remove from the oven. Add parsley, cheese, and salsa to the baking sheet, then stir to combine.
    1 ⅓ cups shredded cheese, Italian blend
    1 ⅓ cup salsa
  11. To serve, divide the cauliflower rice bake and ham steaks between plates. Enjoy!
