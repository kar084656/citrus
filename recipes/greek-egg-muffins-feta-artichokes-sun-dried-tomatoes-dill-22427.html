Greek Egg Muffins with Feta, Artichokes, Sun-Dried Tomatoes & Dill

Ingredients

  • 2 (6 oz) jarsartichoke hearts, marinated
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 8eggs
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • 4sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • butter, unsalted
  • crushed red pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F and position a rack in the lower third of the oven. Grease 8 muffin cups with butter; place muffin pan on a baking sheet pan (to catch any overflow during baking) and set aside.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  2. Wash, dry, and shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
  3. Mince sun-dried tomatoes. Drain and roughly chop artichokes. Add both to the bowl with the dill, along with the feta, and stir to combine the filling.
    4 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    2 (6 oz) jars artichoke hearts, marinated
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  4. Divide filling between muffin cups. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
  5. Crack eggs into the reserved bowl. Add crushed red pepper, then whisk to combine. Pour eggs over the filling.
    8 eggs
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. Place muffin pan in the oven and bake, rotating the pan halfway through, until eggs are set, about 15 minutes. Remove from the oven and let cool for 1 minute. Use a silicone spatula to wedge each egg muffin out of its cup.
  7. To serve, divide egg muffins between plates and enjoy!
