Ingredients

  • 64 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • 1 ½ lblean ground pork
  • 4 capsportobello mushrooms
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • sesame seeds
  • soy sauce
  • toasted sesame oil

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 caps portobello mushrooms
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 medium zucchini squash
  2. Remove stems and scrape out the gills of the mushrooms; small dice, place in a medium bowl, and set aside.
  3. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  4. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork, salt, and pepper; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 ½ lb lean ground pork
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger and garlic; add both to the bowl with the mushrooms.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. When the pork is done, add mushrooms, ginger, and garlic to the pot. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are soft and fragrant, 3-4 minutes.
  7. Add broth and soy sauce to the pot; stir to combine, cover, and bring the soup to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until flavors blend, about 5 minutes.
    64 fl oz (8 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 tbsp soy sauce
  8. Meanwhile, trim zucchini and use a julienne (or regular) peeler to make noodles.
  9. When the soup is done, add zucchini noodles to the pot, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  10. To serve, divide soup between bowls, drizzle with oil, and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
    4 tsp sesame seeds
