Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Place vinegar, honey, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    ⅓ cup rice vinegar
    2 tsp honey
    1 tsp salt
  3. Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside.
    1 medium red onion
  4. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 yellow bell peppers
    2 pints grape tomatoes
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  5. Trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips. Transfer to a large baking sheet pan.
  6. Peel garlic. Add to the baking sheet with the peppers along with the tomatoes. Drizzle veggies with oil, then season with salt and crushed red pepper; stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    12 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  7. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are browned and fork-tender, 20-25 minutes.
  8. About 15 minutes before the veggies are done, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 ½ lb ribeye steak
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  10. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, about 5 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves.
  12. When the veggies are done, remove from the oven. Add basil and pickled onion to the baking sheet, then stir to combine.
  13. Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  14. To serve, divide steak and veggies between plates. Enjoy!
