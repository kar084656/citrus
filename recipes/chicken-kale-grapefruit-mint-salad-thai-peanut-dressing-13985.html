Chicken, Kale, Grapefruit & Mint Salad with Thai Peanut Dressing

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 1 small pkgfresh mint
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2grapefruits
  • 2 buncheskale
  • ½ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • fish sauce
  • honey
  • natural peanut butter
  • rice vinegar
  • salt
  • soy sauce

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 grapefruits
    2 bunches kale
    1 small pkg fresh mint
  2. Cut off skin and white pith from grapefruits. Slice between the membranes to release segments, transfer to a small bowl, and set aside.
  3. Peel and mince garlic; place in a large bowl, along with oil, peanut butter, vinegar, honey, fish sauce, and soy sauce. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp natural peanut butter
    4 tsp rice vinegar
    4 tsp honey
    2 tsp fish sauce
    2 tsp soy sauce
  4. Pat chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    1 ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Working in batches if necessary, add the chicken and grill until cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side; remove from heat and set aside.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces and transfer to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the kale and toss to combine the salad.
  9. Roughly chop peanuts.
    ½ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  10. Thinly slice chicken into strips.
  11. To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Top with grapefruit segments, grilled chicken, and peanuts. Enjoy!
