Ingredients

  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
  • 1 ½ cupsfrozen corn
  • 4green bell peppers
  • 8 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • 2jalapeño peppers
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 8small flour tortillas
  • 2 mediumyellow onions
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell peppers. Cut into ½-inch thick strips and transfer to a large baking sheet pan.
    4 green bell peppers
  3. Wash, dry, and halve jalapeño peppers lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Slice into thin strips and add to the baking sheet with the bell pepper. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
    2 jalapeño peppers
  4. Drizzle the mixed peppers with oil and season with spices; toss to coat, then spread out in an even layer.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, tossing halfway, until peppers are soft and begin to brown, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  7. While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and thinly slice onions into half-moons.
    2 medium yellow onions
  8. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft, 4-5 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. Meanwhile, place corn in a colander and run under hot water (from the tap) to thaw; set aside to drain.
    1 ½ cups frozen corn
  10. Cut cream cheese into small pieces.
    ½ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  11. Add cream cheese, yogurt, cream, and broth to the skillet with the onion; cook, stirring frequently, until the sauce is smooth, 1-2 minutes.
    1 cup plain Greek yogurt
    8 fl oz (1 cup) heavy whipping cream
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  12. When the peppers are done, add them to the skillet along with the corn; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
  13. Meanwhile, warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    8 small flour tortillas
  14. Divide creamy veggie sauté between bowls; serve with warm tortillas on the side for dipping. Enjoy!
