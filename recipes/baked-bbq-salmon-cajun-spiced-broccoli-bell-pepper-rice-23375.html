Ingredients

  • 1 (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
  • 1 cupjasmine rice
  • 2red bell peppers
  • 1 ½ lbsalmon fillet
  • barbecue sauce
  • Cajun seasoning
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup jasmine rice
    2 cup water
  3. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, pat salmon dry with paper towels. Place on one half of a large baking sheet pan and spread barbecue sauce over top.
    1 ½ lb salmon fillet
    ¼ cup barbecue sauce
  5. Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell peppers; halve crosswise, then slice into thin strips. Add bell pepper and broccoli to the other half of the baking sheet.
    2 red bell peppers
    1 (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
  6. Drizzle veggies with oil, then sprinkle with Cajun seasoning and salt. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Cajun seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
  7. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until veggies are fork-tender and salmon is cooked through, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. To serve, divide rice, salmon, and veggies between plates. Enjoy!
