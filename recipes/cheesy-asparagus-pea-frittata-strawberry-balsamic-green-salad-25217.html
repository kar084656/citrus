Ingredients

  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 2 small bunchesasparagus
  • 90 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
  • 12eggs
  • 2 cupsfrozen peas
  • 1 cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    2 small bunches asparagus
    1 (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. Preheat a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. While the skillet heats up, snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears into bite-sized pieces.
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add asparagus, peas, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until asparagus softens, about 5 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 cups frozen peas
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. Meanwhile, place eggs in a large bowl; whisk to combine and set aside.
    12 eggs
  6. Preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  7. When the asparagus is soft, reduce heat to medium-low; pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, 3-5 minutes.
  8. Sprinkle cheese over the frittata. Place skillet in the oven and broil until top is set and cheese is melted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
    1 cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  9. Meanwhile, trim and thinly slice strawberries.
  10. To serve, cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Add mixed greens to the plate, top with strawberries and almonds, then drizzle with vinaigrette. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced
    ⅓ cup balsamic vinaigrette
