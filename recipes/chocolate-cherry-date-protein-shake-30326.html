Cookware

Ingredients

  • 1 cupfrozen cherries
  • ½ cupmedjool dates, pitted
  • 16 fl ozwhole milk
  • cinnamon, ground
  • cocoa powder
  • salt
  • vanilla extract
  • vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)

Steps

  1. Not all protein powders are created equal. If desired, adjust shake consistency by adding more or less liquid per package instructions. This recipe will make about 8 cups of shake. Work in batches to best fit your blender.
  2. Place milk, water, protein powder, cherries, dates, cocoa powder, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt in a blender. Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) whole milk
    1 ⅓ cup water
    4 servings vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
    1 cup frozen cherries
    ½ cup medjool dates, pitted
    ¼ cup cocoa powder
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. To serve, divide shake between glasses, adding ice if desired. Enjoy!
