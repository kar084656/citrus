Ingredients

  • ½ cupCaesar dressing
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1lime
  • ½ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ⅔ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
  • 1 (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 4whole grain pitas
  • honey

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 lime
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  2. Trim and halve strawberries; place on a plate and set aside.
  3. Zest and juice lime into a small bowl; add yogurt and honey. Whisk to combine the dip and set aside.
    ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp honey
  4. Halve tomatoes and place in a medium bowl.
  5. Halve lettuce lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  6. Place chicken on a cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces and add to the bowl.
    1 (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  7. Add Parmesan and dressing to the bowl. Stir to combine the chicken salad filling.
    ½ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    ½ cup Caesar dressing
  8. Using a clean cutting board, cut pitas in half and carefully open the pockets. Stuff each pocket with the filling.
    4 whole grain pitas
  9. To serve, divide pitas, strawberries, and dip between plates. Enjoy!
