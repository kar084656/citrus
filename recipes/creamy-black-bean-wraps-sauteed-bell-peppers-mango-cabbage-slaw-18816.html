Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 small bunch cilantro
    2 limes
    1 jalapeño pepper
    1 mango
    1 small head red cabbage
    2 red bell peppers
  2. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half in a large bowl and half in a blender.
  3. Juice one lime into the bowl with the cilantro; juice remaining lime into the blender and set aside.
  4. Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl with the cilantro. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  5. Peel and mince shallot; add to the bowl along with oil, salt, and cumin; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 shallot
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
  6. Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. Quarter the cabbage lengthwise, then cut out and discard the core; slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips and add to the bowl. Stir to combine the slaw and set aside.
  8. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. While the skillet heats up, trim, seed, and thinly slice bell peppers.
  10. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add bell pepper and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  11. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans; add to the blender with the cilantro and lime juice.
    2 (15 oz) cans black beans
  12. Peel garlic; add to the blender along with water, pumpkin seeds, and spices. Blend on high until smooth.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 cup water
    ½ cup pumpkin seeds
    2 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    1 tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp salt
  13. Place tortillas on a flat surface and spread each with an even layer of black bean spread. Top with bell peppers and a small portion of slaw (reserving the rest for serving); roll tightly to close.
    4 large flour tortillas
  14. Cut wraps in half and divide between plates; serve with remaining slaw on the side and enjoy!
