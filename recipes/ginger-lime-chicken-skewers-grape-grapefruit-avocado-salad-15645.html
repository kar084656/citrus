Steps

  1. If using wooden skewers, place 8-12 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    2 limes
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    ½ lb red grapes
    2 grapefruits
    1 avocado
    2 (5 oz) pkgs spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. Zest limes into a medium bowl (set limes aside for later use).
  4. Peel and mince shallots; place half in the bowl with the lime zest (set remaining shallot aside).
    2 shallots
  5. Peel and grate or mince ginger. Add to the bowl with the lime zest along with mayo, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the marinade.
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces; transfer to the bowl with the marinade, stir to coat, and set aside.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  7. Meanwhile, juice limes into a large bowl. Add remaining shallot, oil, fish sauce, sugar, and Dijon; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp fish sauce
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  8. Thread chicken onto skewers and place on a plate; alternatively, you can cook the chicken pieces directly on the grill pan without using skewers.
  9. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. Once the grill pan is hot, working in batches if necessary, add the skewers and cook, turning occasionally, until chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
  11. While the chicken is cooking, using a clean cutting board, halve grapes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. Cut off skin and white pith from the grapefruits. Slice between the membranes to release segments; cut into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl.
  13. Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon. Add to the bowl along with the mixed greens and toss to combine the salad.
  14. To serve, divide salad and skewers between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
