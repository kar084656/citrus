Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 40 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 16 ozham steak, boneless
  • 8 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ¼ cuphoney mustard dressing
  • 1 lbnew potatoes
  • 2pears
  • ⅔ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • butter, unsalted
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lb new potatoes
    2 pears
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. Cut potatoes into quarters, then slice into ⅛-inch thick pieces. Place in a medium bowl and set aside.
  3. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  4. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    1 medium yellow onion
  5. Once the pot is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, Italian seasoning, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    4 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
  6. Add potatoes and broth to the pot. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer. (Reserve bowl for the salad.)
    40 fl oz (5 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  7. While the soup is heating, small dice ham.
    16 oz ham steak, boneless
  8. Once the soup is simmering, add ham, stir to combine, and simmer until potatoes are fork-tender, 8-10 minutes.
  9. Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, quarter, core, and thinly slice pears. Place in the reserved bowl.
  10. When the potatoes are tender, add cream and cheese; stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the cheese is melted and soup is heated through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) heavy whipping cream
    ⅔ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  11. Add spinach and dressing to the bowl with the pear. Toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup honey mustard dressing
  12. Divide soup between bowls and serve with salad on the side. Enjoy!
