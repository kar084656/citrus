Ingredients

  • 64 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 16 ozdeli roast beef
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • 4 cupsfrozen shredded hash brown potatoes
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 3 cupssauerkraut
  • 1 (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • thyme, dried

Steps

  1. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  2. While the pot heats up, wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces.
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add half of the green onions (reserving the rest for serving), shredded carrots, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies soften, 3-4 minutes.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    1 tsp thyme, dried
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  4. Meanwhile, drain sauerkraut in a colander.
    3 cups sauerkraut
  5. When the carrots have softened, add sauerkraut, broth, and shredded potatoes to the pot; stir to combine the stew and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes.
    64 fl oz (8 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 cups frozen shredded hash brown potatoes
  6. Meanwhile, stack roast beef slices one on top of another, then medium dice.
    16 oz deli roast beef
  7. When the potatoes are tender, add roast beef and peas to the stew. Stir to combine and continue to cook until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    1 cup frozen peas
  8. To serve, divide stew between bowls. Top with reserved green onions and enjoy!
