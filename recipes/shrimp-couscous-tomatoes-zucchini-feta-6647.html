Ingredients

  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 1 small bunchfresh dill
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2 lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • oregano, dried
  • virgin coconut oil

Steps

  1. In a small saucepan, bring broth to a boil over high heat. Remove from heat, add couscous, and stir. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes then fluff with a fork.
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 cup whole wheat couscous
  2. If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    2 lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  3. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 small bunch fresh dill
    2 medium zucchini squash
    2 yellow bell peppers
  4. Halve tomatoes. Cut peppers into quarters, remove seeds and membranes and slice into strips. Medium dice the zucchini into ½-inch cubes. Transfer cut veggies into a medium bowl.
  5. Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
  8. Add the shrimp and garlic to the skillet; cook until shrimp are opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  9. Return skillet to the heat, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add peppers, zucchini, tomato, and oregano to the skillet, stirring occasionally until vegetables soften, 3-4 minutes.
    4 tsp virgin coconut oil
    1 tsp oregano
  10. Shave dill leaves from stalk; discard stalk and mince leaves.
  11. Once veggies are soft add broth, scraping up any browned bits, and cook until most of the liquid has evaporated, 4-5 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  12. Add dill to the skillet, along with the shrimp and any accumulated juices; allow to cook for 1 minute more.
  13. To serve, divide couscous between plates and top with the shrimp, veggies, and crumbled feta. Enjoy!
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
