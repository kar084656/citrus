Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    2 avocados
    1 small bunch cilantro
  2. Peel, trim, and small dice carrots. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the carrot.
  4. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  5. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add to the bowl.
    1 medium yellow onion
  6. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    1 tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  7. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans. Place in the reserved bowl and set aside.
    2 (15 oz) cans pinto beans
  8. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain. When the veggies are soft, add rice to the pot; cook, stirring constantly, until rice is slightly translucent, 1-2 minutes.
    1 cup basmati rice
  9. Add beans, tomatoes, and broth to the pot. Stir to combine and bring to a boil over high heat.
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  10. Once the liquid in the pot comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the pot, and reduce heat to low. Simmer until rice is tender and the liquid is mostly absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  11. When the rice is almost done, halve and pit avocados; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and thinly slice. Set aside for serving.
  12. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half of the cilantro to the rice and stir to combine (reserve the rest for serving).
  13. To serve, divide rice between plates or bowls; top with avocado and reserved cilantro. Enjoy!
