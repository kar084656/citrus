Chicken Soba Noodle Salad with Edamame, Cilantro & Peanut Dressing

Ingredients

  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1 ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 1 small bunchcilantro
  • 1 cupedamame, shelled
  • 1English cucumber
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 2red bell peppers
  • 10 ozsoba (buckwheat) noodles
  • black pepper
  • honey
  • natural peanut butter
  • rice vinegar
  • salt
  • soy sauce
  • toasted sesame oil

Steps

  1. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets.
    1 ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  2. Place chicken, salt, and pepper in a medium saucepan and cover with water by 1-inch; bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer until chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes; transfer chicken to a plate to cool.
    1 tsp salt
    1 tsp black pepper
  3. Meanwhile, fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  4. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 red bell peppers
    2 medium carrots
    1 English cucumber
    1 small bunch cilantro
  5. Once the water in the pot is boiling, add noodles and edamame; cook noodles according to package directions, about 5 minutes. Once done, drain and rinse thoroughly under cold water; set aside.
    10 oz soba (buckwheat) noodles
    1 cup edamame, shelled
  6. While the noodles and edamame cook, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a large bowl along with soy sauce, vinegar, oil, peanut butter, and honey; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 cloves garlic
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    2 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp natural peanut butter
    2 tsp honey
  7. Trim, seed, and thinly slice bell peppers; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots; add to the bowl.
  9. Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
  10. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  11. Once the chicken is cool, shred using two forks; use one fork to hold the chicken and the other fork to pull and shred the meat. Add to the bowl along with noodles and edamame.
  12. Roughly chop peanuts and add to the bowl; toss to combine the noodle salad.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  13. To serve, divide noodle salad between plates or bowls and enjoy!
