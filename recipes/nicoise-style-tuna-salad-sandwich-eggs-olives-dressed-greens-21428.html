Ingredients

  • 1 (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • 2 headsbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 2 tbspcapers
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • ¼ cupFrench vinaigrette
  • 4hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  • ⅔ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 160 mlsun-dried tomato pesto
  • 4whole grain buns or rolls
  • mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Wash and dry lettuce, then separate the leaves; set aside one leaf per sandwich and chop or tear remaining leaves into bite-sized pieces. Place in a large bowl.
    2 heads butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. Add broccoli slaw to the bowl with the lettuce, toss to combine the salad, and set aside.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  3. Drain tuna, place in a medium bowl, and flake with a fork.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  4. Roughly chop eggs and add to the bowl with the tuna.
    4 hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  5. Mince olives and capers; add to the bowl.
    ⅔ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    2 tbsp capers
  6. Add pesto and mayo to the bowl, then stir to combine the sandwich filling.
    ⅔ cup sun-dried tomato pesto
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
  7. To assemble sandwiches, layer reserved lettuce leaves and filling on the bottom half of each roll; close with the top half.
    4 whole grain buns or rolls
  8. To serve, divide sandwiches and salad between plates. Drizzle salad with vinaigrette and enjoy!
    ¼ cup French vinaigrette
