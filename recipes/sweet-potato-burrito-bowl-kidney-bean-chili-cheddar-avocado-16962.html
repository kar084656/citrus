Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a large baking sheet pan with aluminum foil and set aside.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 ½ lb sweet potatoes
    2 red bell peppers
    1 avocado
  3. Peel and cut sweet potatoes lengthwise into 1-inch thick wedges (4-6 pieces per potato, depending on size), then transfer to the baking sheet. Drizzle with oil and season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    1 tsp salt
  4. Place sweet potato wedges in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until fork-tender, about 25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans
  6. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  8. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, and corn; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 cup frozen corn
  9. While the veggies are cooking, trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers.
  10. Add beans, bell pepper, tomatoes, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine the chili and bring to a simmer.
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    4 tsp chili powder
    2 tsp cumin, ground
    1 tsp paprika
    1 tsp salt
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  11. Once the chili is simmering, reduce heat to medium and cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender and sauce has thickened, 5-6 minutes. Remove from heat.
  12. Meanwhile, coarsely grate cheese.
    ½ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  13. Halve and pit avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out with a spoon.
  14. To serve, divide sweet potato wedges between plates or bowls; top with chili, cheese, and avocado. Enjoy!
