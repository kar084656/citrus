Ingredients

  • 1 (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 1English cucumber
  • ¼ cupFrench vinaigrette
  • 4 cupsfrozen butternut squash, pieces
  • 2Gala apples
  • 10 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil. Uncover, add butternut squash, pasta, and salt; stir for a few seconds. Cook until pasta reaches desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    4 cup frozen butternut squash, pieces
    10 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
    1 tsp salt
  3. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    2 Gala apples
    1 English cucumber
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  4. Quarter, core, and thinly slice apples. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the apple and set aside.
  6. When the pasta and butternut squash are done, drain and transfer to a large baking dish.
  7. Add Alfredo sauce and spices to the baking dish with the pasta and butternut squash. Stir to combine.
    1 (16 oz) jar alfredo sauce
    1 tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  8. Place dish in the oven and bake until sauce is warmed through and the top is golden, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. Just before serving, add spinach, pumpkin seeds, and vinaigrette to the bowl with the apple and cucumber. Toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
    ¼ cup French vinaigrette
  10. To serve, divide pasta bake and salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
