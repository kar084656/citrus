Portobello Mushroom Egg Bakes with Asparagus & Parmesan Quinoa

Ingredients

  • 2 small bunchesasparagus
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 8eggs
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • 8 capsportobello mushrooms
  • 1 cupquinoa
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and place the rack in the middle position.
  2. In a small saucepan, combine the quinoa and broth; bring to a boil.
    1 cup quinoa
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. Wash and dry the mushrooms; remove the stems and carefully scrape out the gills, making sure not to damage the rim of the mushrooms.
    8 caps portobello mushrooms
  4. Place the mushrooms on the baking sheet; rub with olive oil.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. Once the quinoa is boiling, reduce heat to a simmer and cover; cook for 15 minutes. Once cooked, remove from the heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
  6. Break an egg into each mushroom cap and season with salt and pepper.
    8 eggs
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. Place the mushrooms in the oven and bake for 8 minutes.
  8. Wash and dry the asparagus. Snap (or cut) off and discard the woody ends; cut the spears on an angle into 1-inch pieces. Transfer to a large bowl, drizzle with olive oil, and season with salt and pepper; toss to coat.
    2 small bunches asparagus
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Finely grate ½ cup of Parmesan.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  10. After the 8 minutes are up, remove the sheet pan from the oven temporarily. Arrange the asparagus on the sheet around the mushrooms.
  11. Return the sheet to the oven and continue to bake until the eggs are cooked and the asparagus is tender, 7 to 10 more minutes.
  12. Uncover the quinoa and fluff with a fork. Add the asparagus and Parmesan; stir until well combined.
  13. Divide the mushrooms and quinoa between four plates (or two plates and two lunch containers). Enjoy!
