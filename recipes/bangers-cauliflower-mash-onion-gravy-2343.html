Ingredients

  • 2 medium headscauliflower
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1 small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 8pork sausages
  • 2 mediumyellow onions
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • cornstarch
  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot about halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. Wash cauliflower and cut off the leaves. Separate into small florets and cut the thick stems into smaller pieces. Transfer to a bowl.
    2 medium heads cauliflower
  3. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Add sausages to skillet and cook until browned on all sides, 2-3 minutes. Reduce heat to medium, add water, and continue cooking until cooked (or heated) through, 6-8 minutes. Alternatively, cook according to package directions. Transfer to a plate.
    8 pork sausages
    ⅛ cup water
  5. Add cauliflower to boiling water and cook until very tender, 6-7 minutes.
  6. Thinly slice onions into half rings.
    2 medium yellow onions
  7. Once sausages have been transferred, add butter and onions to skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until onions are softened and caramelized, about 10 minutes.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  8. Peel and mince garlic.
    4 cloves garlic
  9. Drain cauliflower in a colander, then return to saucepan. Add water, garlic, butter, salt, and pepper. Blend using a hand/immersion blender or a potato masher. Cover and set aside.
    ¼ cup water
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  10. Sprinkle onions with cornstarch and mix until cornstarch is evenly incorporated with the onions.
    2 tsp cornstarch
  11. Pour in broth and cook until mixture is thickened, 4-5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. Wash and dry parsley. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and chop the leaves.
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  13. Return sausages to skillet on top of the onion gravy and turn until heated through.
  14. To serve, place cauliflower purée in a shallow bowl. Add sausages and top with onion gravy. Garnish with parsley and enjoy!
