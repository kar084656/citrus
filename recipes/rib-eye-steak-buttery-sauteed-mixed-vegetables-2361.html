Ingredients

  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1 lbgreen beans
  • 2red bell peppers
  • 1 ½ lbribeye steak
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil

Steps

  1. Wash green beans, carrots, and bell peppers.
    1 lb green beans
    2 medium carrots
    2 red bell peppers
  2. Trim green bean ends and transfer to a large bowl. Seed and slice peppers into strips lengthwise; add to bowl. Trim ends, peel, and thinly slice carrots on the diagonal; add to bowl.
  3. Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Season steak with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 ½ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Coat grill or skillet with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  6. Heat a separate skillet over medium heat.
  7. Peel and mince garlic.
    4 cloves garlic
  8. Melt butter in skillet and add garlic; cook until fragrant, 15-30 seconds. Add vegetables and cook, stirring often, until tender-crisp, 5-6 minutes. Season with salt and pepper; toss to coat.
    4 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Serve steak with sautéed vegetables on the side. Enjoy!
