Pork Chop, Dijon Roasted Cabbage & Arugula-Pear Salad with Blue Cheese

Ingredients

  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • 2pears

  • 1 mediumred onion

  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 small head red cabbage
    2 pears
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. Place Dijon, oil, water, honey, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the Dijon sauce.
    2 tbsp Dijon mustard
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp water
    2 tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. Halve cabbage lengthwise and cut out the core; slice crosswise into 1-inch thick wedges. Transfer to a large baking sheet pan, then drizzle both sides of the cabbage with Dijon sauce. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  5. Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, gently flipping cabbage halfway through, until tender and golden around the edges, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels. Place on a plate and season both sides with salt and pepper.
    4 pork chops, boneless
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  8. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. While the chops are cooking, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, and salt in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
  10. Peel, halve, and slice onion into thin half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine.
    1 medium red onion
  11. Quarter, core, and thinly slice pears; add to the bowl.
  12. Roughly chop walnuts and add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  13. Just before serving, add arugula and blue cheese to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  14. To serve, divide roasted cabbage, chops, and salad between plates. Enjoy!
