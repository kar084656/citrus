Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • ¼ cupdried cranberries
  • 6 cupsfrozen butternut squash, pieces
  • ¼ cuphoney mustard dressing
  • ½ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ cuppumpkin seeds
  • 1 (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • paprika
  • salt

Steps

  1. Place butternut squash, broth, coconut milk, paprika, and salt in a large pot. Stir to combine the soup.
    6 cup frozen butternut squash, pieces
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    1 tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
  2. Bring the soup to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until squash is very soft, 8-10 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, wash and dry arugula (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Place in a medium bowl.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  4. Add carrots, Parmesan, cranberries, and pumpkin seeds to the bowl with the arugula. Set aside.
    1 (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ½ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    ¼ cup dried cranberries
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
  5. When the soup is done, remove from the heat. Purée with an immersion or regular blender (return to the pan).
  6. Add dressing to the bowl with the arugula and toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup honey mustard dressing
  7. Divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with additional pumpkin seeds; serve with salad on the side. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
