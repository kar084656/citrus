Ingredients

  • 2bananas
  • ½ cupdried cranberries
  • 2eggs
  • ½ cuppecan halves
  • cinnamon, ground
  • honey
  • oats, quick cooking

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and place the rack in the center position. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper (optional).
  2. Peel bananas, break into chunks, and place in a medium bowl.
    2 bananas
  3. Crack the eggs into the bowl and add honey. Use a fork or potato masher to mash the ingredients together.
    2 eggs
    2 tbsp honey
  4. Roughly chop the pecans and add to the bowl.
    ½ cup pecans, dry roasted
  5. Add oats, cinnamon, and cranberries to the bowl. Stir until all ingredients are well combined.
    2 ⅔ cup oats, quick cooking
    1 tsp cinnamon
    ½ cup dried cranberries
  6. Use a ¼ cup measure to evenly scoop the batter onto the sheet pan. Press each cookie flat with your fingertips. Place in the oven and bake for 12 minutes.
  7. Allow to cool for a few minutes before transferring to plates, enjoy!
