Ingredients

  • 2 (15 oz) cansblack beans
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 48 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • 1 small pkgfresh thyme
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1lemon
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    2 sticks celery
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 small pkg fresh thyme
    1 lemon
  2. Drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    2 (15 oz) cans black beans
  3. Peel and medium dice carrots and potatoes; place both in a medium bowl.
  4. Trim celery and cut crosswise into thin pieces; add to the bowl with the carrot and potato.
  5. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  6. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the other veggies.
    1 medium yellow onion
    4 cloves garlic
  7. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add veggies, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  8. Meanwhile, trim and medium dice zucchini.
  9. Slide thyme leaves off the stems; discard the stems.
  10. When the veggies are soft, add beans, zucchini, thyme, broth, coconut milk, and corn to the pot. Stir to combine the soup, cover, and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat.
    48 fl oz (6 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    1 cup frozen corn
  11. Once the soup is simmering, reduce heat to medium-low and cook, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are fork-tender, about 8 minutes. Remove from heat.
  12. Juice lemon into the soup and stir to combine.
  13. To serve, divide soup between bowls and enjoy!
