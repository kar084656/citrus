Curried Chicken, Chickpea & Cauliflower Bake with Cilantro Raita

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 small bunchcilantro

  • 2 (15 oz) cansgarbanzo beans (chickpeas)

  • honey
  • rice vinegar
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Place vinegar, honey, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    ⅓ cup rice vinegar
    1 ½ tsp honey
    1 tsp salt
  3. Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside.
    1 medium red onion
  4. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium head cauliflower
    1 English cucumber
    2 limes
    1 small bunch cilantro
  5. Drain and rinse chickpeas. Transfer to a large baking sheet pan and pat dry with paper towels.
    2 (15 oz) cans garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  6. Separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add to the baking sheet with the chickpeas.
  7. Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the baking sheet, drizzle with oil, then season with curry powder and salt. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 ½ lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp curry powder
    1 tsp salt
  8. Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, stirring halfway through, until chickpeas are crisp, cauliflower is fork-tender, and chicken is cooked through, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel, trim, and grate cucumber. Use paper towels or a clean cloth to squeeze excess moisture out of the grated cucumber. Place in a medium bowl.
  10. Juice one lime into the bowl with the cucumber. Cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  11. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with yogurt, additional honey, crushed red pepper, and salt; stir to combine the raita.
    2 cups plain Greek yogurt
    4 tsp honey
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
    ½ tsp salt
  12. Divide chicken bake between plates or bowls; top with pickled onion. Serve with raita on the side and enjoy!
