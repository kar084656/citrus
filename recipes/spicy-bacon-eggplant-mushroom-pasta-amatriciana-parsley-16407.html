Ingredients

  • 8 slicesbacon
  • 2eggplants

  • 1 small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • 10 ozpenne pasta
  • ½ mediumred onion

  • ½ lbwhite mushrooms
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz penne pasta
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 eggplants
    ½ lb white mushrooms
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. Trim and small dice eggplants; quarter mushrooms. Transfer both to a medium bowl and set aside.
  4. Small dice bacon.
    8 slices bacon
  5. Preheat another large pot over medium-high heat.
  6. Once the pot is hot, add the bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 5-6 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate, leaving the drippings in the pot.
  7. While the bacon cooks, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince onion and garlic.
    ½ medium red onion
    4 cloves garlic
  8. Return the pot (with bacon drippings) to medium heat. Once the pot is hot, add eggplant, mushrooms, onion, garlic, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until eggplant and mushrooms are soft and golden, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
  9. When the pasta is done, drain in a colander and return to the pot; cover and set aside.
  10. Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  11. Finely grate Parmesan.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  12. When the veggies are done, add tomato sauce and spices to pot. Stir to combine the veggie sauce and bring to a simmer; cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, about 3 minutes.
    2 (8 oz) cans tomato sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  13. Add veggie sauce, bacon, and half of the parsley (reserving the rest for garnish) to the pot with the pasta; stir to combine.
  14. To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls; top with reserved parsley, additional crushed red pepper, and Parmesan. Enjoy!
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
