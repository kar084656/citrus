Ingredients

  • 2 ½ lbchicken drumsticks
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2limes
  • 1 smallpineapple
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 2shallots

  • crushed red pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • soy sauce
  • thyme, dried

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 limes
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    1 small pineapple
    1 red bell pepper
  2. Peel and mince ginger, garlic, and shallots. Place in a medium bowl along with soy sauce, oil, and spices; stir to combine the rub.
    4 cloves garlic
    2 shallots
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  3. Pat chicken dry with paper towels and add to the bowl with the rub; turn to coat.
    2 ½ lb chicken drumsticks
  4. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or large skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Working in batches if necessary, add chicken and grill, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 15-20 minutes. Remove to a plate and cover with foil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 400°F.
  7. Juice limes into another medium bowl.
  8. Trim green onions; cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle. Add to the bowl with the lime juice.
  9. Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  10. Cut off skin from pineapple, core, and cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  11. Seed and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  12. Spread coconut on a baking sheet pan, place in the oven, and toast until golden, 2-3 minutes.
    ½ cup shredded unsweetened coconut
  13. To serve, divide chicken and salad between plates. Sprinkle salad with toasted coconut and enjoy!
