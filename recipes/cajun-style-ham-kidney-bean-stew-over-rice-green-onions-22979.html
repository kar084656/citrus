Ingredients

  • 1 cupbasmati rice
  • 2 (14.5 oz) cansdiced tomatoes
  • 2green bell peppers
  • 2 small bunchesgreen onions (scallions)
  • 16 ozham steak, boneless
  • 2 (15 oz) canskidney beans
  • all-purpose flour
  • butter, unsalted
  • Cajun seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup basmati rice
    2 cup water
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell peppers; small dice. Place in a medium bowl.
    2 green bell peppers
  4. Wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add half to the bowl with the bell pepper. (Reserve remaining green onion for serving.)
    2 small bunches green onions (scallions)
  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, small dice ham. Add to the bowl with the veggies.
    16 oz ham steak, boneless
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, ham, flour, and Cajun seasoning. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies start to soften, about 3 minutes.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour
    4 tsp Cajun seasoning
  8. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans.
    2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans
  9. When the veggies are soft, add beans, tomatoes, and salt to the skillet. Stir to combine the stew and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover with a lid, and cook until beans are tender and stew has thickened, 8-10 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    ½ tsp salt
  10. To serve, divide rice and stew between plates or bowls. Sprinkle with reserved green onion and enjoy!
