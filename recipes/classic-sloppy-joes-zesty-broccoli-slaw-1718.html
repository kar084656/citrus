Ingredients

  • 1 (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1 ½ lblean ground beef
  • 2red bell peppers
  • 2 (8 oz) canstomato sauce
  • 4whole grain buns or rolls
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • honey
  • red wine vinegar
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • Worcestershire sauce

Steps

  1. Peel and small dice onion. Wash, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Peel and mince garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 red bell peppers
    2 cloves garlic
  2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add onion and peppers. Stirring frequently, cook until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  4. Add beef and garlic. Breaking apart with a spoon, cook until meat is browned and crumbly, 2-3 minutes.
    1 ½ lb lean ground beef
  5. Add tomato sauce, vinegar, honey, Worcestershire, Dijon, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Stirring occasionally, reduce heat to low and simmer until sauce thickens, 10-15 minutes.
    2 (8 oz) cans tomato sauce
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    1 tbsp honey
    1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. Peel and mince garlic. In a medium bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together garlic, olive oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper.
    2 cloves garlic
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. Add broccoli slaw to bowl with dressing and toss to coat.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  8. Toast buns or rolls if desired.
    4 whole grain buns or rolls
  9. To serve, fill buns or rolls with sloppy joe mixture and enjoy with broccoli slaw on the side!
