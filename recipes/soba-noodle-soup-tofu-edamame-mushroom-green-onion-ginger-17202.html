Ingredients

  • 64 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 1 ⅓ cupsedamame
  • 2 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 10 ozsoba (buckwheat) noodles
  • soy sauce
  • toasted sesame oil

Steps

  1. Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    2 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. Thinly slice mushrooms and place in a medium bowl.
  4. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  5. While the pot heats up, peel and mince ginger and garlic; add both to the bowl with the mushrooms.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms, ginger, and garlic; cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms are lightly browned, 5-6 minutes.
    2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  7. Meanwhile, cut tofu into ½-inch cubes.
  8. When the mushrooms are done, add broth to the pot and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
    64 fl oz (8 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  9. Once the broth is boiling, add tofu, noodles, edamame, and soy sauce; stir to combine the soup. Bring to a simmer over medium heat and continue to cook until noodles are tender and tofu is warmed through, 5 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    10 oz soba (buckwheat) noodles
    1 ⅓ cups edamame
    ¼ cup soy sauce
  10. Meanwhile, trim green onions and cut crosswise into thin pieces; add to the soup and stir to combine.
  11. To serve, divide soup between bowls and enjoy!
