Ingredients

  • 1 small bunchcilantro
  • 1 cupguacamole
  • 30 mlItalian salad dressing
  • 1lime
  • ½ cupsalsa
  • 1 cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 4veggie burger patties, plant-based
  • 4whole grain buns or rolls
  • chili powder
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 small bunch cilantro
    1 lime
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook until golden brown on one side, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 veggie burger patties, plant-based
  4. Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. Juice lime into the bowl with the cilantro. Add mayo, dressing, and chili powder; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tbsp Italian salad dressing
    ¼ tsp chili powder
  6. Flip the burger patties, top with cheese, and continue to cook until cheese is melted and patties are golden brown and cooked through, 4-5 minutes more (or cook patties according to package instructions). Remove to a plate.
    1 cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
  7. Meanwhile, toast buns if desired.
    4 whole grain buns or rolls
  8. Add mixed greens to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
  9. Place bottom half of each bun on a plate, then layer with a cheesy burger patty, guacamole, and salsa; close with the top half of each bun. Serve with salad on the side. Enjoy!
    1 cup guacamole
    ½ cup salsa
