Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. (Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water before draining.)
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and crushed red pepper; cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant and golden, about 1 minute.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. Add tomatoes and salt to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until simmering. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low, and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 12-15 minutes more.
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Place in a small bowl along with ricotta and salt, then stir to combine. Set aside.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
    1 (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. Wash, dry, zest, and juice lemon into a medium bowl.
    1 lemon
  8. Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the lemon along with oil, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 shallot
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Wash and dry arugula (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  10. Add drained pasta and reserved pasta water to the skillet with the sauce. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until heated through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  11. To serve, divide pasta and arugula between plates or bowls. Top pasta with ricotta and crushed red pepper. Enjoy!
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
