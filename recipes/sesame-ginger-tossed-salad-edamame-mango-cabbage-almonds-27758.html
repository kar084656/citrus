Ingredients

  • ⅔ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 2avocados
  • 3 cupsedamame

  • 1mango
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
  • 160 mlsesame ginger dressing
  • 1 (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
  • sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 hearts romaine hearts
    1 English cucumber
    1 mango
    2 avocados
  2. Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Place in a large bowl.
  3. Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the lettuce.
  4. Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  5. Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; add to the bowl.
    3 cups edamame
  6. Add shredded cabbage, almonds, and dressing to the bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    1 (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
    ⅔ cup almonds, sliced
    ⅔ cup sesame ginger dressing
  7. Halve and pit avocados; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice crosswise into thin half-moons.
  8. To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with avocado and sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    4 tsp sesame seeds
