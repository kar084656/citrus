Steps

  1. Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Once the water in the pot is boiling, remove from heat and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes, or according to package directions, then drain in a colander and rinse under cold water; set aside.
    10 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  3. Meanwhile, place soy sauce, fish sauce, water, honey, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    2 tbsp fish sauce
    2 tbsp water
    4 tsp honey
    1 tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp clove, ground
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
  4. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 medium carrots
    2 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 small bunch cilantro
  5. Peel and mince ginger; place in a medium bowl along with chicken, additional soy sauce, and pepper. Using your hands, mix well to combine.
    1 lb ground chicken
    2 tsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. Using a tablespoon measure, form the chicken mixture into rounded meatballs and place on a plate.
  7. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add meatballs and cook, turning occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through, 6-8 minutes. Transfer to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet with cooking juices for later use.)
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  9. Meanwhile, peel, trim, and slice carrots crosswise into thin rounds; halve snap peas crosswise. Place both in a clean medium bowl.
  10. Trim and slice green onions crosswise into ¼-inch pieces; add to the bowl with the other veggies.
  11. Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and stir-fry until fork-tender, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  12. While the veggies are cooking, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  13. When the veggies are done, add noodles, sauce, and cilantro to the skillet; cook, stirring frequently, until the noodles are coated and warmed through, 2-3 minutes.
  14. Meanwhile, roughly chop cashews.
    ¼ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
  15. To serve, divide noodles, veggies, and meatballs between bowls; top with cashews and enjoy!
