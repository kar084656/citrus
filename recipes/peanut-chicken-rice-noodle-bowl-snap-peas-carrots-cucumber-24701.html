Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 1English cucumber
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 160 mlpeanut sauce

  • salt
  • soy sauce
  • toasted sesame oil

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Remove from heat, uncover, and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes or according to package directions. Drain in a colander and rinse under cold water.
    10 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    1 English cucumber
  3. Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. Halve snap peas crosswise. Add to the bowl with the green onions and set aside.
  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces. Season with salt and pepper.
    1 ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden and almost cooked through, 3-4 minutes.
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  8. Add green onions and snap peas to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies are tender and chicken is cooked through, 3-4 minutes.
  9. Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons.
  10. When the chicken and veggies are done, add peanut sauce, water, and soy sauce to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce is warmed through and thickened slightly, 2-3 minutes more.
    ⅔ cup peanut sauce
    ¼ cup water
    2 tbsp soy sauce
  11. Add drained noodles to the skillet, toss to combine, and remove from heat.
  12. To serve, divide saucy noodles, chicken, and veggies between bowls; top with cucumber and carrots. Enjoy!
    1 (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
