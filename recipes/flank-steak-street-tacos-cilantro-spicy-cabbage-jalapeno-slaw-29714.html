Ingredients

Steps

  1. Place soy sauce, oil, and spices in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the marinade.
    4 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    4 tsp chili powder
    2 tsp cumin, ground
    1 tsp salt
  2. Pat steak dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board. Slice crosswise into thin strips, then cut each strip into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the marinade, stir to coat, and set aside for 15 minutes.
    1 ½ lb flank steaks
  3. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 small bunch cilantro
    2 limes
    2 jalapeño peppers
    1 medium head napa cabbage
  4. Using a clean cutting board, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half in another medium bowl. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
  5. Juice limes into the bowl with the cilantro. Add oil and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ½ tsp salt
  6. Halve jalapeño peppers lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Slice crosswise into thin half-moons and add to the bowl with the dressing. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw; set aside
  8. When the steak is done marinating, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. Once the skillet is hot, add steak and cook, undisturbed, until browned, 2-3 minutes. Turn and continue to cook until desired doneness, 1-2 minutes more. Transfer to a plate and set aside to rest, about 5 minutes.
  10. Meanwhile, peel, halve, and mince onion. Set aside for serving.
    1 medium red onion
  11. Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    12 small corn tortillas
  12. To serve, divide tortillas and slaw between plates. Top tortillas with steak, hot sauce, onion, and reserved cilantro. Enjoy!
    4 tsp hot sauce
