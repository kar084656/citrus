Ingredients

  • 2avocados
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula

  • 1 ½ lbsalmon fillet
  • 1 cupsalsa
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. While the pan heats up, pat salmon dry with paper towels. Place on a plate and season the top side with salt and pepper.
    1 ½ lb salmon fillet
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add salmon, skin-side down, and cook, flipping once, until skin is crispy and flesh is opaque, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. Meanwhile, place corn in a colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Transfer to a medium bowl.
    2 cups frozen corn
  5. Wash, dry, halve, and pit avocados; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and small dice. Add to the bowl with the corn, along with the salsa, and stir to combine; set aside.
    2 avocados
    1 cup salsa
  6. Wash and dry arugula (skip this step if it came pre-washed).
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  7. To serve, divide salmon, salsa, and arugula between plates; drizzle arugula with vinaigrette. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup French vinaigrette
