Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 1 small bunchcilantro
  • 4flatbreads or naan
  • 8hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  • 1 (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • brown sugar
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garam masala
  • garlic powder
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  2. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    1 medium yellow onion
  3. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. Add marinara, water, spices, and sugar to the pot. Stir to combine the curry and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 6-8 minutes.
    1 (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    1 cup water
    4 tsp garam masala
    1 tsp garlic powder
    1 tsp salt
    1 tsp brown sugar
  5. Meanwhile, halve eggs and place in a small bowl; set aside.
    8 hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  6. Wash and dry spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed).
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  7. Wash and dry cilantro. Shave leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
    1 small bunch cilantro
  8. Once the curry has thickened, add spinach and cilantro to the pot in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  9. Add eggs to the curry, gently stir to combine, and continue to cook until warmed through, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  10. Meanwhile, toast naan if desired, then cut into wedges.
    4 flatbreads or naan
  11. Divide curry between bowls and serve with naan on the side. Enjoy!
