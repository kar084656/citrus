Fresh Creamed Corn Fettuccine with Lettuce, Cucumber & Tomato Salad

Ingredients

  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 earscorn
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcream cheese

  • salt

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Reserve 1 cup cooking water before draining; set aside.
    2 tsp salt
    10 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    4 ears corn
    1 English cucumber
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. Remove husks and silks from the corn. Using a knife, carefully slice corn kernels off the cobs and place in a medium bowl.
  4. Peel and mince garlic; peel and small dice half the onion (reserving the rest for later use). Add both to the bowl with the corn.
    4 cloves garlic
    1 medium red onion
  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add corn, garlic, onion, broth, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until corn starts to soften and break down, 8-10 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tsp salt
  7. Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, and spices in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp white wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. Thinly slice remaining onion. Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise; slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add both to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl; set aside.
  10. Cut cream cheese into small pieces. Finely grate Parmesan.
    ½ (8 oz) block cream cheese
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. When the corn is ready, add cream cheese and Parmesan to the skillet. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until cheese melts and the sauce thickens slightly, 2-3 minutes.
  12. Transfer pasta to the skillet and toss to combine; add reserved pasta water, a few tablespoons at a time, if necessary, to reach a saucy consistency. Remove from heat.
  13. Add mixed greens to the bowl with the veggies and toss to combine the salad.
  14. To serve, divide pasta and salad between plates. Enjoy!
