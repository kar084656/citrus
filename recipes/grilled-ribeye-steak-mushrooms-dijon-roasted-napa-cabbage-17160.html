Ingredients

  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 medium headnapa cabbage
  • 1 ½ lbribeye steak
  • 1 lbwhite mushrooms
  • apple cider vinegar
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 475°F.
  2. Peel and mince garlic; place in a small bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the Dijon sauce and set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. Wash and dry cabbage, then trim off and discard the root end. Quarter cabbage lengthwise; transfer to a baking sheet pan, cut-side down.
    1 medium head napa cabbage
  4. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, turning each cabbage wedge onto the other cut-side halfway through, until starting to soften, 10-12 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, cut the steak into portions, pat dry with paper towels, and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 ½ lb ribeye steak
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. Meanwhile, wash, dry, and, using a clean cutting board, thinly slice mushrooms.
    1 lb white mushrooms
  9. When the cabbage is soft, remove baking sheet from the oven and turn wedges once more, so the cut-sides face up; drizzle with Dijon sauce.
  10. Set oven to broil on high and return baking sheet to the oven. Broil until cabbage is golden brown and lightly charred around the edges, about 3 minutes.
  11. When the steak is done, return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring often, until soft and golden, about 4 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. To serve, divide steak and roasted cabbage between plates. Top steak with mushrooms and enjoy!
