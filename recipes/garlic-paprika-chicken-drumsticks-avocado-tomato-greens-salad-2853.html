Ingredients

  • 2avocados
  • 2 ½ lbchicken drumsticks
  • 6 clovesgarlic
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2limes
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • paprika
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 500°F. Line a baking dish with foil.
  2. Peel and mince or press the garlic; transfer to a medium bowl.
    6 cloves garlic
  3. Add lime juice, olive oil, paprika, salt, and pepper to the minced garlic and mix well.
    1 lime
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp paprika
    1 tsp salt
    1 tsp black pepper
  4. Add the chicken drumsticks to the garlic-paprika mixture and toss until well coated. Place the drumsticks in the baking dish.
    2 ½ lb chicken drumsticks
  5. Place the drumsticks in the oven and bake, flipping halfway through cooking time, until they are cooked through and browned, 25-30 minutes.
  6. In a small bowl, prepare the dressing by whisking together lime juice, olive oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper.
    1 lime
    4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. Wash and dry the mixed greens.
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  8. Wash and halve the tomatoes.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  9. Halve and pit the avocados; scoop out the flesh and medium dice.
    2 avocados
  10. To serve, arrange the greens on plates, top with tomatoes and avocado, and drizzle with the dressing. Add chicken drumsticks to the side. Enjoy!
