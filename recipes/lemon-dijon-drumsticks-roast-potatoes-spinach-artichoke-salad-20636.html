Ingredients

  • 2 (6 oz) jarsartichoke hearts, marinated
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 2 ½ lbchicken drumsticks
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1lemon
  • 1 lbnew potatoes
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • mayonnaise
  • salt
  • white wine vinegar

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a large baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make clean up easier (optional).
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lemon
    1 lb new potatoes
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. Peel and mince garlic; place in a large bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. Juice lemon into the bowl with the garlic, then add oil, Dijon, and spices; whisk to combine the marinade.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. Quarter potatoes and add to the bowl with the marinade.
  6. Pat chicken dry with paper towels, add to the bowl, and toss to coat. Transfer potatoes and drumsticks to the baking sheet, arranging them in an even layer; drizzle any leftover marinade over top.
    2 ½ lb chicken drumsticks
  7. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning halfway through, until potatoes and chicken are cooked through, 25-30 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. Meanwhile, place mayo, oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Drain and thinly slice artichokes; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    2 (6 oz) jars artichoke hearts, marinated
  10. Right before serving, add spinach to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  11. To serve, divide chicken, potatoes, and salad between plates. Enjoy!
