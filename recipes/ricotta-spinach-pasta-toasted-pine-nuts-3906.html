Ricotta & Spinach Pasta with Toasted Pine Nuts

Ingredients

  • 2 (5 oz) pkgsbaby spinach
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; small dice the onion (cut into ¼-inch pieces) and transfer to a small bowl.
    1 medium red onion
  3. Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add to the bowl with the onion.
    4 cloves garlic
  4. Finely grate 1 cup of Parmesan. Transfer to a small bowl.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  5. Preheat a sauté pan over medium heat.
  6. When the water in the saucepan comes to a boil, uncover the pan and season the water with salt, then add the pasta and stir for a few seconds. Cook the pasta, uncovered, for 11 to 12 minutes (or according to package directions). Once cooked, drain the pasta in a colander, reserving 1 cup of the cooking water.
    2 tbsp salt
    10 oz (about 3 cups) penne pasta
  7. Add the pine nuts to the pan; cook, stirring constantly, until they are golden brown, 3 to 5 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl.
    ¼ cup pine nuts
  8. Once the pine nuts have been transferred, add olive oil to the pan and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. Add the onion and garlic to the pan; cook, stirring frequently, until the onion is softened, 3 to 5 minutes.
  10. Add the spinach to the pan and season the mixture with salt and pepper; cook, tossing frequently, until the spinach is wilted, 1 to 2 minutes.
    2 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  11. Add the ricotta to the pan and stir until it is thoroughly mixed with the spinach.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg (1 cup) ricotta cheese
  12. Add the pasta, about ½ of the Parmesan, and reserved cooking water to the pan; cook, stirring constantly, until the pasta is well combined, 1 to 2 minutes.
  13. To serve, divide the pasta between bowls, garnish with the remaining Parmesan, and sprinkle with the toasted pine nuts. Enjoy!
