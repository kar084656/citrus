Steps

  1. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup jasmine rice
    2 cup water
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    8 tomatoes
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. While the rice is cooking, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. Halve tomatoes lengthwise, then cut into ½-inch thick wedges. Add to the bowl with the ginger and garlic, then set aside.
  6. Crack eggs into another medium bowl. Add salt, then beat with a whisk or fork until smooth.
    12 eggs
    ½ tsp salt
  7. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. While the skillet heats up, trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces.
  9. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add green onions (reserving a small handful for serving) and cook, stirring frequently, until beginning to soften, about 30 seconds.
    2 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
  10. Add eggs to the skillet and cook, stirring frequently, until just set, about 3 minutes. Remove to a plate. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
  11. Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tomatoes and soy sauce; cook, stirring occasionally, until beginning to soften, about 3 minutes.
    2 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ cup soy sauce
  12. Meanwhile, place water, ketchup, cornstarch, and sugar in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    ¼ cup water
    ¼ cup ketchup
    2 tsp cornstarch
    2 tsp brown sugar
  13. Once the tomatoes have softened, reduce heat to medium. Add sauce and continue to cook, stirring frequently, until sauce thickens slightly, 2-3 minutes.
  14. Return eggs to the skillet, using a spoon to break them up. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  15. To serve, divide rice and stir-fry between plates or bowls. Top with reserved green onions and sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    4 tsp sesame seeds
