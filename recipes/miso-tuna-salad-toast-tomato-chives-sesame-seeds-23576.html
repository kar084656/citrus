Ingredients

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  2. Place mayo, miso paste, vinegar, sugar, garlic powder, and pepper in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the dressing and set aside.
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    2 tbsp miso paste, white
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    ½ tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 stick celery
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 small pkg chives
  4. Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  5. Drain tuna, add to the bowl, and flake with a fork. Stir to combine the tuna salad and set aside.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  6. Arrange bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    4 slices whole grain bread
  7. Meanwhile, halve tomatoes lengthwise. Chop chives into small pieces.
  8. Divide tuna salad between toast slices and spread out in an even layer. Top with tomatoes, chives, and sesame seeds.
    1 tsp sesame seeds
  9. To serve, divide tuna salad toast between plates and enjoy!
