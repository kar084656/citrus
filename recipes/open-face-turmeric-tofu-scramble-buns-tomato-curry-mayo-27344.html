Steps

  1. Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    2 (12 oz) pkgs extra firm tofu
  2. Place mayo and spices in a small bowl. Stir to combine the curry mayo and set aside.
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    1 tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 green bell peppers
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    2 tomatoes
  4. Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper and green onion; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. Crumble the tofu with your fingers into the skillet, then season with spices. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until tofu is warmed through and golden, 3-4 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Meanwhile, thinly slice tomatoes.
  10. Toast buns if desired.
    4 whole grain buns or rolls
  11. To serve, divide buns between plates and spread with curry mayo; top with tomato slices and tofu scramble. Enjoy!
