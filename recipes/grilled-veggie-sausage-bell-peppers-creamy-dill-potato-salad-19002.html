Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 medium yellow potatoes
    4 orange bell peppers
    2 sticks celery
    1 small bunch fresh dill
  2. Peel and medium dice potatoes; place in a medium saucepan, along with salt, and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, and simmer until fork-tender, 10-15 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
  3. Meanwhile, trim, seed, and quarter bell peppers. Place in a large bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper; toss to combine.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. Once the grill pan is hot, working in batches if necessary, add peppers (reserve bowl for the next step) and cook, turning occasionally, until soft, 7-8 minutes. Transfer peppers to a plate and cover with aluminum foil (reserve grill pan for later use).
  6. Meanwhile, place mayo, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. Trim and small dice celery; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. Peel and mince shallot; add to the bowl.
    1 shallot
  10. Return grill pan to medium heat. Once the pan is hot, coat with oil; add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until warmed through, about 5 minutes (or cook according to package instructions). Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 pre-cooked vegan sausage
  11. When the potatoes are done, drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Add potatoes to the bowl with the dressing and veggies; stir to combine the potato salad.
  12. Slice cooked peppers into bite-sized pieces.
  13. To serve, divide potato salad, peppers, and sausages between plates. Enjoy!
