Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 small pkg fresh basil
    1 lemon
    2 (5 oz) pkgs baby spinach
    2 avocados
    4 tomatoes
  2. Mince olives and sun-dried tomatoes; transfer both to a small bowl along with oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the tapenade and set aside.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    8 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. Pour broth into a large pot, add spices, and bring to a boil over medium heat. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer.
    64 fl oz (8 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp nutmeg, ground
  4. Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; transfer to a medium bowl.
  5. Finely grate Parmesan; add to the bowl with the basil (setting a small handful aside for garnish).
    4 oz Parmesan cheese
  6. Juice lemon into the bowl.
  7. Add eggs to the bowl and whisk until well blended; set aside.
    8 eggs
  8. Roughly chop spinach.
  9. Add spinach to the simmering broth in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  10. Slowly pour the egg mixture into the soup, while gently stirring, to separate into ribbons. Simmer until the egg is cooked through, 2-3 minutes; cover and remove from heat.
  11. Meanwhile, halve and pit the avocados. Slice thinly while still in skin and scoop out with a spoon. Thinly slice tomatoes.
  12. Arrange avocado and tomato slices on plates and spoon tapenade over top.
  13. Divide soup between bowls and top with reserved Parmesan. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!
