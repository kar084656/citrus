Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap) and bring to a boil. Remove from heat and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes or according to package directions; drain in a colander and rinse.
    10 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 crowns broccoli
    2 medium carrots
    2 red bell peppers
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    1 lime
    1 small bunch cilantro
  3. Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain.
    2 cups edamame
  4. Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Place in a large bowl.
  5. Peel, trim, and cut carrots crosswise into ¼-inch thick rounds. Add to the bowl with the broccoli.
  6. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. While the skillet heats up, trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; add to the bowl.
  8. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, salt, and pepper to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp, about 5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Peel and small dice onion.
    4 cloves garlic
    ½ medium red onion
  10. When the veggies are done, return them to the reserved bowl.
  11. Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic, ginger, and onion to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, about 2 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  12. Add coconut milk, water, fish sauce, honey, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine the sauce and bring to a simmer.
    1 (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ½ cup water
    2 tbsp fish sauce
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp turmeric, ground
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
  13. Once the sauce is simmering, add drained rice noodles, edamame, and cooked veggies to the skillet; stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until noodles are warmed through and sauce has thickened slightly, 3-4 minutes.
  14. Meanwhile, cut lime into wedges; set aside for serving.
  15. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add cilantro to the skillet, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  16. To serve, divide saucy noodles and veggies between bowls; top with a squeeze of lime and enjoy!
