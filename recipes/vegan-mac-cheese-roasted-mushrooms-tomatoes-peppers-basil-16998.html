Vegan "Mac & Cheese" with Roasted Mushrooms, Tomatoes, Peppers & Basil

Ingredients

  • 12 fl ozalmond milk, unsweetened
  • 1 lbcremini mushrooms
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 2 pintsgrape tomatoes
  • 2green bell peppers
  • 10 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • ⅔ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • arrowroot starch/powder

  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • onion powder
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Remove from heat and drain in a colander. (Reserve pot for later use.)
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  3. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lb cremini mushrooms
    2 green bell peppers
    2 pints grape tomatoes
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  4. Thinly slice mushrooms and transfer to a large baking sheet pan.
  5. Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; add to the baking sheet with the mushrooms.
  6. Add tomatoes to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices; toss to combine, then spread out in an even layer.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp garlic powder
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, tossing once, until veggies are tender and tomatoes start to burst, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. When the pasta is done, place almond milk, nutritional yeast, oil, arrowroot starch, Dijon, onion powder, and garlic powder in the reserved pot; whisk until smooth. Place the pot over medium heat and bring the sauce to a simmer, whisking occasionally.
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) almond milk, unsweetened
    ⅔ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp arrowroot starch/powder
    2 tbsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
  9. Once the sauce is simmering, continue to cook, whisking constantly, until the sauce becomes thick and stretchy, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  10. Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  11. Return drained pasta to the pot with the cheese sauce, add half of the basil (reserving the rest for serving), and stir to combine.
  12. If needed, warm the mac & cheese over medium heat, stirring constantly, until heated through, 1-2 minutes.
  13. To serve, divide mac & cheese between plates or bowls; top with roasted veggies and reserved basil. Enjoy!
