Scallop Fettuccine Alfredo with Lemon, Parsley & Sautéed Asparagus

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and set aside.
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 small bunches asparagus
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 lemon
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. While the skillet heats up, snap (or cut) off woody ends of asparagus.
  5. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add asparagus, salt, and pepper; cook, turning occasionally, until bright green and tender-crisp, 3-4 minutes. Transfer to a plate and cover with aluminum foil to keep warm. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Place in a small bowl, setting aside a small handful for serving.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  7. Return skillet to medium heat. Add Parmesan, cream, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until cheese melts and sauce is combined, 1-2 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) heavy whipping cream
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. While the sauce is cooking, pat the scallops dry with paper towels and place on another plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 ½ lb sea scallops
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Add scallops to the skillet with the sauce and gently stir to coat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until scallops are opaque, about 5 minutes.
  10. Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  11. When the scallops are done, juice lemon into the skillet. Add drained pasta and parsley. Reduce heat to low and continue to cook, tossing occasionally, until sauce thickens, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  12. To serve, divide scallop fettuccine and asparagus between plates. Top pasta with reserved Parmesan and enjoy!
