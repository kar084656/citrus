Steps

  1. If using wooden skewers, place 8-12 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place in a large bowl.
    1 ½ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  3. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    4 peaches
    4 tomatoes
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  4. Zest and juice lemon into the bowl with the shrimp. Add oil, salt, and pepper; toss to combine and set aside to marinate.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. Meanwhile, peel and mince the garlic; place in another large bowl along with oil, vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 cloves garlic
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. Peel and thinly slice the shallot; add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine.
    1 shallot
  7. Cut regular tomatoes in half and slice into thick half-moons; halve the grape tomatoes. Add both to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. Meanwhile, quarter the peaches (around the pit) and twist to separate, then remove and discard the pits.
  10. Working in batches if necessary, place the peaches on the pan and grill until charred, 2 minutes per side. Remove to the bowl with the tomatoes and toss gently to combine the salad.
  11. While the peaches are cooking, thread the shrimp onto skewers. If you don't have skewers you can cook the shrimp directly on the pan. Discard the marinade.
  12. Working in batches if necessary, add the shrimp to the pan and grill until opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  13. Tear mozzarella into large chunks with your hands.
    1 (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  14. Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  15. To serve, divide shrimp and Caprese salad between plates. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with basil. Enjoy!
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
