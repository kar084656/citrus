Ingredients

  • 2Gala apples
  • 2 clovesgarlic

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 Gala apples
    6 medium zucchini squash
  3. Peel and mince ginger, garlic, and shallots; transfer to a small bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 shallots
  4. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. While the skillet heats up, quarter, core, and small dice apples; add to the bowl with the ginger, garlic, and shallot.
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add ginger, garlic, shallot, and apple. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden and soft, 5-6 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  8. Trim zucchini and quarter lengthwise, then halve crosswise; arrange on a large baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil, season with spices, and sprinkle with Parmesan.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until zucchini is fork-tender, 12-15 minutes; remove from oven.
  10. When the apple is soft, add broth, vinegar, sugar, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine the chutney and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened, about 10 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. When the chutney is done, transfer to the reserved bowl and set aside. Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels and return to medium-high heat.
  12. While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board; thinly slice crosswise into bite-sized strips, then season with salt and pepper.
    4 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  13. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add pork and stir-fry until browned and cooked through, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  14. Meanwhile, add yogurt to the chutney and stir to combine.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  15. Using a clean cutting board, cut pitas in half and carefully open the pockets; stuff each pocket with creamy chutney and pork.
    4 whole grain pitas
  16. To serve, divide pitas and zucchini between plates. Enjoy!
