Spicy Carrot Flatbread with Arugula, Apricots, Almonds & Minted Yogurt

Ingredients

  • ¼ cupalmonds
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 6 mediumcarrots
  • 12dried apricots
  • 4flatbreads or naan
  • 1 small pkgfresh mint

  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    6 medium carrots
    1 small pkg fresh mint
    1 lime
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. Peel, trim, and slice carrots into ¼-inch thick rounds. Place in a large bowl along with maple syrup, oil, and salt; toss to coat.
    4 tsp pure maple syrup
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  4. Transfer carrots to a large baking sheet pan and spread out in a single layer. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing halfway through, until fork-tender, about 12 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  5. While the carrots are roasting, arrange flatbreads on another large baking sheet. Add to the oven and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, about 8 minutes. Remove from the oven and set aside to cool.
    4 flatbreads or naan
  6. Meanwhile, pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl along with yogurt and salt; stir to combine and set aside.
    1 ⅓ cups plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. Peel and mince the garlic. Transfer to the reserved bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  8. Juice lime into the bowl with the garlic; add oil, maple syrup, salt, and crushed red pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. Thinly slice apricots and add to the bowl with the dressing.
    12 dried apricots
  10. Roughly chop almonds and set aside.
    ¼ cup almonds
  11. Once the carrots have cooled to room temperature, transfer to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. Add arugula to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  13. Divide minted yogurt between flatbreads and spread out in an even layer. Top each flatbread with carrot-arugula salad and slice into wedges.
  14. To serve, divide flatbreads between plates. Sprinkle with almonds and drizzle with vinegar. Enjoy!
    4 tsp balsamic vinegar
