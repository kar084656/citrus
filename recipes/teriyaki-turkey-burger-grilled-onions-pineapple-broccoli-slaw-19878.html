Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    1 small pineapple
    6 baby bok choy
  2. Peel and mince ginger and garlic; place both in a small saucepan.
    4 cloves garlic
  3. Add broth, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, and chili-garlic sauce to the saucepan with the ginger and garlic; whisk to combine. Place over high heat and bring to a boil.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    4 tsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  4. Meanwhile, in a small bowl, make a slurry by whisking water with cornstarch until smooth. Once the liquid in the saucepan comes to a boil, add slurry in a steady stream while whisking quickly. Let boil for 1 minute, then remove teriyaki sauce from heat and set aside.
    ¼ cup water
    2 tbsp cornstarch
  5. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the pan heats up, peel and slice onion crosswise into thin rings. Transfer to a plate, drizzle with oil, and season with salt on both sides.
    1 medium red onion
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. Once the pan is hot, add the onions and grill, flipping once, until tender and lightly charred, 3-4 minutes. Return onions to the plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve pan for the burger patties.)
  8. Meanwhile, place turkey, bread crumbs, salt, pepper, and half of the teriyaki sauce in a medium bowl; mix with your hands until well combined. Form the turkey mixture into 4 equal-sized patties and place on another plate. (Reserve remaining teriyaki sauce for serving.)
    1 ½ lb ground turkey
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  9. When the onions are done, return the pan to medium-high heat and coat with oil. Add burger patties and cook, undisturbed, for 5 minutes; flip patties, and cook an additional 5-6 minutes, until cooked through. Add to the plate with the onions and re-cover with foil.
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. While the patties are cooking, place additional oil, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, and chili-garlic sauce in a clean medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    4 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  11. Cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and medium dice. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. Trim and thinly slice bok choy crosswise into shred-like strips; add to the bowl, along with the broccoli slaw, and toss to combine.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  13. Toast buns if desired.
    4 whole grain buns or rolls
  14. Place bottom half of each bun on a plate; layer with burger patties, remaining teriyaki sauce, onions, and a small portion of the slaw. Close with the top half of each bun and serve with remaining slaw on the side. Enjoy!
