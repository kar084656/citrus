Ingredients

  • 2 (15 oz) cansblack beans
  • 1 small bunchcilantro
  • 1 ½ lbcod fillet
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 1English cucumber

  • black pepper

  • honey
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make clean-up easier (optional).
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 lemons
    2 red bell peppers
    1 English cucumber
    1 small bunch cilantro
  3. Zest and juice lemons into a medium bowl; add oil, honey, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp honey
    1 tsp garlic powder
    1 tsp coriander, ground
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  4. Pat fish dry with paper towels and transfer to the baking sheet; drizzle with half of the dressing and turn to coat. (Set remaining dressing aside for the salad.)
    1 ½ lb cod fillet
  5. Place fish in the oven and broil until the fish flakes easily with a fork, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans in a colander; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    2 (15 oz) cans black beans
  7. Place corn in the colander, run under hot water (from the tap) to defrost, and add to the bowl.
    1 cup frozen corn
  8. Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; add to the bowl.
  9. Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
  10. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with the feta, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the salad.
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  11. To serve, divide fish and salad between plates. Enjoy!
