Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. In a medium saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    1 cup quinoa
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. Meanwhile, place oil and spices in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the spice oil.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 tsp curry powder
    1 tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 medium head cauliflower
    1 small pkg fresh mint
    1 English cucumber
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  5. Separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add to the bowl with the spice oil and stir to coat. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer.
  6. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until cauliflower is fork-tender, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. While the cauliflower is roasting, pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half of the mint in a small bowl. (Reserve remaining mint for serving.)
  8. Add yogurt, tahini, water, vinegar, additional garlic powder, and salt to the bowl with the mint. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ cup tahini
    3 tbsp water
    2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
  9. Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces.
  10. Roughly chop pistachios.
    1 cup pistachios, unsalted
  11. To serve, divide quinoa, cauliflower, arugula, and cucumber between bowls. Drizzle with sauce, then top with pistachios and reserved mint. Enjoy!
