Breadcrumb-Crusted Halibut with Mushroom-Spinach Sauté & Quinoa

Ingredients

  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1 lbhalibut fillet
  • 1lemon
  • 1 cupquinoa
  • 1 lbwhite mushrooms
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • panko bread crumbs
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    1 cup quinoa
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. In a small bowl, mix together lemon juice, bread crumbs, dill, salt, and pepper.
    1 lemon
    6 tbsp panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp dill
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. Place halibut in a small baking dish. Drizzle with a little olive oil and top with bread crumb mixture, patting lightly to help it adhere.
    1 lb halibut fillet
    about 2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. Place dish in the oven and bake until fish is white and flaky, 10-12 minutes. Change setting to broil and continue baking until bread crumb mixture turns golden brown, 1-2 minutes.
  6. Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  7. Peel and mince garlic. Wash, dry, and thinly slice mushrooms.
    4 cloves garlic
    1 lb white mushrooms
  8. Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, 15-30 seconds. Add mushrooms and season with salt and pepper. Stirring occasionally, cook until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    about 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Wash and dry spinach. Add to mushrooms and toss until just wilted, 1-2 minutes.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  10. Uncover quinoa and fluff with a fork.
  11. To serve, place quinoa on a plate, add spinach-mushroom sauté, and top with halibut fillet. Enjoy!
