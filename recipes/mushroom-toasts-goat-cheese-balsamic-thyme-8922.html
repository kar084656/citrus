Ingredients

  • ½ small pkgfresh thyme
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (4 oz) logsgoat cheese
  • 2shallots
  • 1 lbwhite mushrooms
  • 8 sliceswhole grain bread
  • balsamic vinegar
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • salt

Steps

  1. Peel and thinly slice the shallots into rings.
    2 shallots
  2. Wash and slice mushrooms.
    1 lb white mushrooms
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the mushrooms and shallot; cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms begin to soften, about 4 minutes.
    ¼ cup butter, unsalted
  6. Preheat oven to broil on high; position rack under the broiler.
  7. Wash and dry thyme. Slide leaves off the stems; discard the stems.
    ½ small pkg fresh thyme
  8. Add garlic, vinegar, salt, and pepper to the skillet and cook, stirring often, until fragrant, 1-2 minutes. Remove from the heat.
    2 tsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Place bread slices on top of a large baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, 2-3 minutes, or until toasted. Remove from the oven.
    8 slices whole grain bread
  10. Spread goat cheese over toast and top with warm mushrooms.
    2 (4 oz) logs goat cheese
  11. To serve, divide toasts between dishes and garnish with thyme. Enjoy!
