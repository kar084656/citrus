Ingredients

  • 1 cupbasmati rice
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 small pkgchives
  • 3 ozParmesan cheese
  • 1 lbwhite mushrooms
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • black pepper
  • cayenne pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 lb white mushrooms
    1 small pkg chives
  2. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add broth, salt, and garlic powder to the pan; bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup basmati rice
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp garlic powder
  3. While the broth is heating, trim and coarsely grate the zucchini.
  4. Once the broth comes to a boil, add zucchini and stir the mixture; cover the saucepan and reduce heat to low. Cook zucchini-rice until liquid is mostly absorbed, about 18 minutes. Once done, remove from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  6. Pat chicken dry with paper towels and place on a large baking sheet pan.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  7. Halve mushrooms and add to the baking sheet with the chicken.
  8. Drizzle chicken and mushrooms with oil and season with spices; toss to coat, then spread out in a single layer.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  9. Place baking sheet in the oven and broil, flipping the chicken halfway through, until chicken and mushrooms are golden brown and cooked through, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. Meanwhile, chop chives into small pieces.
  11. Finely grate Parmesan.
    3 oz Parmesan cheese
  12. When the zucchini-rice is done, add chives and Parmesan to the pan and stir to combine.
  13. To serve, divide chicken, mushrooms, and zucchini-chive rice between plates. Enjoy!
