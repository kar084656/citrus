Ingredients

  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • 40 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 small pkgchives
  • 1 (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 1 cuppolenta (cornmeal)
  • 1red bell pepper

  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a large baking sheet pan with parchment paper.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 lb sweet potatoes
    2 crowns broccoli
    1 red bell pepper
    1 small pkg chives
  3. Peel and small dice sweet potatoes; transfer to the baking sheet.
  4. Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; add to the baking sheet.
  5. Peel and medium dice onion; add to the baking sheet.
    1 medium red onion
  6. Drizzle oil over veggies and season with salt and pepper; toss to coat, then spread out in an even layer. Place in the oven and bake until veggies are fork-tender, about 25 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. Pour broth into a medium saucepan, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat.
    40 fl oz (5 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  8. Meanwhile, trim, seed, and mince bell pepper; add to the pan with the boiling broth.
  9. Reduce heat to low and gradually add polenta to the pan in a thin steady stream, while whisking constantly, until the polenta is incorporated into the broth. Cook, stirring occasionally, until grains are soft and the texture is creamy, 15-25 minutes.
    1 cup polenta (cornmeal)
  10. Meanwhile, crumble goat cheese into a small bowl.
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
  11. Chop chives into small pieces.
  12. When the polenta is done, add oil, goat cheese, and half of the chives to the pan and stir until smooth.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. To serve, divide polenta and veggies between bowls. Top with remaining chives and enjoy!
