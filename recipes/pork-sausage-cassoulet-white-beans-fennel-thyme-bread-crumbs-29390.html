Ingredients

  • 2 (15 oz) canscannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 2fennel bulbs
  • 1 small pkgfresh thyme
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 4pork sausages
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • 2 (8 oz) canstomato sauce
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • oregano, dried
  • panko bread crumbs
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 fennel bulbs
    2 yellow bell peppers
    1 small pkg fresh thyme
  3. Trim off root end and stalks of the fennel, leaving only the white bulbs. Halve fennel bulbs lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers. Add to the bowl with the fennel.
  5. Peel, halve, and mince onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    1 medium red onion
    2 cloves garlic
  6. Preheat a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. While the skillet heats up, slide thyme leaves off the stems; discard the stems. Add half to the bowl with the veggies. Mince remaining thyme leaves, place in a small bowl, and set aside for later use.
  8. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggie-thyme mixture and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ½ tsp rosemary, dried
  9. Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans.
    2 (15 oz) cans cannellini (white kidney) beans
  10. When the veggies are done, add beans, tomato sauce, and water to the skillet. Stir to combine, then nestle sausages into the stew.
    2 (8 oz) cans tomato sauce
    1 cup water
    4 pork sausages
  11. Place skillet in the oven and bake, turning sausages and stirring beans halfway through, until sausages are golden and cooked through, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  12. Meanwhile, add bread crumbs to the bowl with the minced thyme and stir to combine.
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
  13. To serve, divide cassoulet between plates or bowls and top with bread crumbs. Enjoy!
