Ingredients

  • 1English cucumber
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 2red bell peppers
  • 1 ½ lbribeye steak
  • 2shallots
  • 2 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • rice vinegar
  • soy sauce
  • toasted sesame oil

Steps

  1. Peel and mince garlic; place in a baking dish along with soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    4 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  2. Pat steak dry with paper towels and add to the baking dish with the marinade; turn to coat and set aside.
    1 ½ lb ribeye steak
  3. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 English cucumber
    2 red bell peppers
    2 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
  4. Peel and mince additional garlic; place in a large bowl along with oil, soy sauce, vinegar, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 cloves garlic
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  5. Peel, halve, and slice shallots into thin half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    2 shallots
  6. Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
  7. Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers; add to the bowl.
  8. Cut snap peas crosswise into ½-inch pieces and add to the bowl; stir to combine the salad and set aside.
  9. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer steak to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  11. Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  12. To serve, divide steak and salad between plates. Enjoy!
