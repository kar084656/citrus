Ingredients

  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 2eggs
  • 1English cucumber
  • ¼ cupFrench vinaigrette
  • 1 small bunchfresh dill
  • 1 ½ lblean ground pork
  • 1 (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
  • 1 (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • panko bread crumbs
  • salt

Steps

  1. Drain and roughly chop roasted peppers. Divide between two medium bowls. (Set one bowl aside for the salad.)
    1 (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  2. Add pork, bread crumbs, eggs, salt, and pepper to the first bowl with the peppers. Use your hands to combine the pork mixture. Using a ¼-cup measure, form the mixture into ½-inch thick patties and place on a plate.
    1 ½ lb lean ground pork
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
    2 eggs
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add patties and cook, flipping once, until golden and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the second bowl with the peppers.
    1 small bunch fresh dill
  6. Wash, dry, trim, and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
    1 English cucumber
  7. Add carrots, almonds, and vinaigrette to the bowl, then stir to combine the salad.
    1 (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced
    ¼ cup French vinaigrette
  8. To serve, divide patties and salad between plates. Enjoy!
