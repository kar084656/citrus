Steps

  1. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add broth and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup basmati rice
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    6 tomatoes
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    1 lime
    1 small bunch cilantro
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  4. If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    1 ½ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  5. Small dice tomatoes; place in a medium bowl and set aside.
  6. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  7. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    1 medium yellow onion
  8. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 3 minutes.
    2 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  9. While the onion cooks, peel and mince ginger and garlic.
    4 cloves garlic
  10. Add ginger, garlic, sugar, and spices to the pot with the onion. Continue to cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp brown sugar
    2 tsp curry powder
    2 tsp chili powder
    2 tsp coriander, ground
    2 tsp cumin, ground
    1 ½ tsp salt
    1 tsp black pepper
    1 tsp turmeric, ground
  11. Add tomatoes, coconut milk, and broth to the pot; stir to combine the curry and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, about 8 minutes.
    1 (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  12. While the curry cooks, cut lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  13. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  14. Once the curry has thickened, add shrimp and half of the cilantro (reserving the rest for garnish) to the pot; stir to combine. Return to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until the shrimp are cooked through, 3-4 minutes.
  15. Add spinach to the pot in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful; stir to combine.
  16. Divide rice and shrimp curry between bowls; sprinkle with reserved cilantro and serve with lime wedges. Enjoy!
