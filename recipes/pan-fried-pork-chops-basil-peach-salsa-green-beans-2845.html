Pan-Fried Pork Chops with Basil Peach Salsa & Green Beans

Ingredients

  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 1 ½ lbgreen beans
  • 1lime
  • 2peaches
  • 4pork chops, boneless
  • 2shallots
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil

Steps

  1. Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. Place the pork chops on a plate, rub both sides with chili powder, and season with salt and pepper. Set aside.
    4 pork chops, boneless
    2 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. Wash the green beans, then trim off and discard the ends.
    1 ½ lb green beans
  4. Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, add the green beans and cook until bright green and tender-crisp, 2-3 minutes. Drain in a colander and rinse under cold water. Set aside to drain further.
  5. Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Add coconut oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the pork chops and cook until browned and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and let them rest for 2-3 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  7. Peel and mince or press the garlic.
    4 cloves garlic
  8. Once the chops have been removed, reduce the heat to medium and add more coconut oil to the skillet. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, 15-30 seconds. Add the green beans and season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, for 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  9. Wash the peaches, halve, core, and small dice; transfer to a medium bowl.
    2 peaches
  10. Peel and mince the shallots; add to the bowl.
    2 shallots
  11. Wash and dry the basil. Pick the leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  12. Drizzle lime juice over the salsa and season with salt and pepper. Mix well.
    1 lime
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. To serve, place pork chops on plates, spoon peach salsa over top, and add green beans to the side. Enjoy!
