Steps

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    10 oz linguine pasta
  2. Meanwhile, cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    2 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  3. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 pints grape tomatoes
    4 medium zucchini squash
    1 small pkg fresh basil
    1 lemon
  4. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. While the skillet heats up, cut the tofu into ¾-inch cubes.
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tofu to the skillet and sprinkle with spices; cook, turning often, until golden on all sides, about 6 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. Meanwhile, halve tomatoes and transfer to a medium bowl.
  8. Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes and set aside.
  9. Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  10. When the tofu is done, remove to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
  11. Return the skillet to medium-high heat and add more oil, swirling to coat the bottom. Add garlic and crushed red pepper; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  12. Add tomatoes, zucchini, broth, Italian seasoning, and salt to the skillet. Stir to combine and bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer until zucchini is soft and sauce has thickened, 6-7 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    1 tsp salt
  13. Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Cut lemon into wedges; set both aside.
  14. Finely grate Parmesan.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  15. When the pasta is done, drain in a colander and return to the pot. Add zucchini-tomato sauce and tofu to the pot; gently toss to combine.
  16. To serve, divide pasta between plates; top with Parmesan, basil, and a squeeze of lemon. Enjoy!
