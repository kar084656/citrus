Steak, Asparagus & Strawberry Salad with Balsamic-Poppy Seed Dressing

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    2 small bunches asparagus
    1 (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 bunch radish
    1 small pkg fresh basil
    2 (5 oz) pkgs spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. Snap or cut off woody ends of the asparagus; cut into bite-sized pieces. Transfer to a large baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and sprinkle with salt and pepper; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until asparagus is tender-crisp, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  5. Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the skillet heats up, pat steak dry with paper towels and season with spices on both sides.
    1 ½ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, sugar, poppy seeds, Dijon, and spices in a large bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    1 tsp poppy seeds
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. Trim and slice strawberries crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. Trim off and discard tops and root ends of radishes; thinly slice and add to the bowl.
  11. Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; add to the bowl.
  12. Add roasted asparagus and mixed greens to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
  13. Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  14. To serve, divide salad between plates. Top with steak and enjoy!
