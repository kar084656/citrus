Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 mangoes
    4 Gala apples
    1 English cucumber
    2 jalapeño peppers
    1 small bunch cilantro
  2. Zest lemon into a medium bowl. (Reserve lemon for later use.)
  3. Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Add to the bowl with the lemon zest, along with yogurt and spices. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 cloves garlic
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tsp garam masala
    2 tsp curry powder
    1 tsp salt
  4. Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Add to the bowl with the marinade and, using your hands, toss to coat.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  5. Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. While the pan heats up, juice reserved lemon into a clean medium bowl. Add oil, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Add chicken and grill until charred and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. Meanwhile, peel mangos and slice flesh away from the pits, starting with the flat sides; roughly chop the flesh. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. Quarter, core, and medium dice apples. Add to the bowl.
  10. Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  11. Thinly slice jalapeño peppers into rings; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  12. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  13. To serve, divide grilled chicken and salad between plates. Enjoy!
